## Research idea

Measurement as Place-Crossing: Completion-Selective Adelic Projection. Conjecture: the quantum measurement problem can be reframed as a completion problem — an adelic quantum state evolves coherently across all completions, and measurement is a place-crossing event in which coupling to a place-specific apparatus projects the state onto a single completion (Archimedean or p-adic). This matters because it would give a structural, number-theoretic account of wavefunction collapse and connect to Dragovich's adelic path integrals and the Langlands correspondence as physics. Testable program: (1) formalize a Hilbert space over the adeles with local factors at each place and a product-formula coherence condition; (2) model apparatus-state coupling as a place-selective operator and derive an effective projection/decoherence equation showing suppression of non-selected completions; (3) simulate a simple adelic harmonic oscillator (Dragovich 2004) coupled to a place-specific bath, checking whether interference across places dies off and whether the selected completion's statistics reproduce Born-rule-like behavior; (4) examine whether UV/IR regularization from p-adic ball spectra follows as a corollary.
