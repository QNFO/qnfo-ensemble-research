## Research idea

Spectral Gap on Bruhat-Tits Trees as Deterministic Primality Test. Claim: encoding an integer N as an automorphism of the (p+1)-regular Bruhat-Tits tree over PGL(2,Q_p) yields a binary spectral signature—discrete, evenly spaced Laplacian eigenvalues (quantized recurrent walk) iff N is prime, continuous diffusive spectrum iff N is composite—giving a deterministic polynomial-time primality test without probabilistic 'liars' like Miller-Rabin. Why it matters: it would replace probabilistic tests, potentially threaten RSA classically, and test the conjecture that spacetime is p-adic/fractal with primes as quantum numbers. How to test: (1) formalize the N-to-automorphism map and prove the spectral-gap dichotomy via Hecke operators and the known spectral theory of PGL(2,Q_p); (2) simulate truncated trees with controlled p-adic precision to check separation of heat-kernel return probabilities for primes vs. composites/Carmichael numbers; (3) derive runtime bounds, targeting polynomial time and comparing against O((log N)^6).
