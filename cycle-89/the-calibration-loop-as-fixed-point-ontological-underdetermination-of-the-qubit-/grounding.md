## Research idea

Qubit Control and Readout as Self-Referential Metrology. The note asks: if engineers reject a billiard-ball picture, what do control pulses actually manipulate and what does readout actually measure — excitations of what medium, and is tomography merely self-referential? The conjecture worth formalizing: qubit control and readout form a closed operational loop whose physical referent (collective mode excitation, spin, circuit degree of freedom) is only fixed by the calibration chain itself, raising the question of whether the qubit ontology is underdetermined by the metrology. A paper could test this by (1) formalizing the calibration hierarchy (drive, Rabi calibration, dispersive readout, tomography) as a self-consistent fixed-point problem, (2) simulating whether distinct physical ontologies (e.g., transmon as anharmonic oscillator vs. two-level abstraction) yield observationally identical tomographic reconstructions, and (3) deriving what, if anything, breaks the circularity — e.g., cross-platform consistency checks or Hamiltonian tomography constraints that pin the referent to a specific substrate excitation.
