## Research idea

Layered Ontology: Brain as Filter Between Phenomenal and Transcendent Layers. The note proposes a three-layer ontology (public-physical, private-phenomenal, transcendent-informational) in which the brain acts as an interface that tightly couples Layer 2 to Layer 1 while filtering or constraining direct awareness of a fundamental informational substrate (Layer 3). The testable conjecture: if consciousness depends on the brain via filtering rather than generation, then altered brain states (near-death, psychedelics, meditation) should systematically reduce filtering, producing predictable phenomenological signatures (loss of self-model, timelessness, unity) that correlate with measurable information-theoretic changes in neural dynamics. A paper could formalize the filter hypothesis as a channel-capacity model, derive predictions distinguishing filtering from production theories, and test them against existing neuroimaging and NDE/psychedelic phenomenology datasets.
