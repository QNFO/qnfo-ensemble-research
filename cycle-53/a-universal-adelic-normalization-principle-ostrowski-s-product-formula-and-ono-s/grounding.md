## Research idea

Universal adelic normalization principle unifying product formula and Tamagawa numbers. Conjecture: Ostrowski's product formula (∏_v |x|_v = 1) and Tamagawa number theory (Ono's formula for τ(T) of algebraic tori) are two instances of one universal adelic normalization principle: local data are individually free, but a global consistency condition forces the product to a canonical value. Test: formalize a common categorical or cohomological framework (e.g., Galois cohomology of character lattices plus idelic integration) from which both the product formula and Ono's τ(T) = |H¹(k,T̂)|/|Sha(T)| can be derived as specializations; then classify which other local-global statements (reciprocity laws, Tate's thesis L-function functional equations) fit the same schema, and identify exactly when the forced global value is 1 versus not.
