## Research idea

Adelic Euler-product cancellation of vacuum energy in ultrametric cosmology. Conjecture: if the total quantum vacuum energy is computed adelically—factored as an Euler product over the real line and all p-adic sectors—the huge positive zero-point contribution from the Archimedean sector is cancelled or neutralized by p-adic contributions, decoupling vacuum expectation value from spacetime curvature and resolving the 10^120 cosmological constant discrepancy. This matters because it would make the worst prediction in physics vanish by topology rather than fine-tuning. Test: derive the vacuum energy of a scalar field (or quantum harmonic oscillator) on a p-adic/Bethe-lattice space, compute the adelic product with the real-sector contribution, and check whether the product converges to a small (observed-scale) value; simulate tree-lattice oscillator ground-state energy distributions to verify hierarchical suppression of UV modes.
