## Research idea

Joint Bruhat-Tits tree unifies mass spectra, QEC, holography, Efimov physics. Conjecture: the product of Bruhat-Tits trees for primes 2, 3, 5, with its diagonal embedding into the Pythagorean semigroup P = {2^a·3^b·5^c}, is a single geometric structure underlying seemingly unrelated phenomena: Standard Model mass ratios (claimed to fit P within ~2%), GKP quantum error correction lattice spacings, holographic bulk geometry, and Efimov log-periodic scaling. Test: (1) statistically assess whether 11+ mass ratios matching P exceeds chance, including a control with random prime triples; (2) derive radiative corrections from p-adic mixing to explain ~1% deviations; (3) simulate photon-loss scaling versus ord_p(k) in GKP codes; (4) check whether Efimov log-periods concentrate on log P. Falsifiable via transmon and entanglement-step experiments.
