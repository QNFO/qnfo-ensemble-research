## Research idea

Time as a Nested Ultrametric Hierarchy of Cycles, Not a Line. The author conjectures that physical time is fundamentally a nested hierarchy of cycles—Compton/Zitterbewegung frequency at the quantum scale, atomic/molecular rhythms, up to cosmological epochs—with the continuous Archimedean time line being only a derived limit as cycle durations tend to zero, and the native geometry of this hierarchy being ultrametric. Why it matters: it would recast mass as tick rate, explain quantum jump durations as cycle resonances, and connect quantum clocks to cyclic cosmology via one structural principle. Testable path: formalize time as an ultrametric space of cycles; derive whether standard dynamics (Dirac equation, quantum jump statistics) emerges as a continuum limit of nested periodic processes; simulate whether ultrametric time reproduces Zitterbewegung interference and predicted jump timescales (~α⁻¹ω_C⁻¹), or derive observable deviations from linear time.
