## Research idea

Geometric resonator comb as engineered non-Markovian bath for qubits. A finite set of resonators with geometrically spaced frequencies ω_k = ω0 q^k and couplings g_k = g0 q^(-γk) synthesizes an effective spectral density J(ω) ∝ ω^(-2γ) that acts as a structured, non-Markovian bath. Claim: the discrete comb's correlation function exhibits long-lived beats causing information backflow, yielding oscillatory rather than exponential decoherence, with partial revivals set by the least-common-multiple rephasing of modes, and a power-law envelope as d grows. This matters because it offers a minimal, chip-implementable model of fractal spectral densities for coherence protection and quantum memory. Test: exact diagonalization or QuTiP mesolve of a qubit coupled to d=4 truncated resonators (pure dephasing and transverse coupling), computing coherence |ρ01(t)| and a trace-distance-based non-Markovianity measure; scan q, γ, temperature (10 mK thermal populations), and detuning to map when backflow survives.
