## Research idea

Time as Bruhat-Tits tree depth: discrete p-adic temporal ordering. The author conjectures that physical time is not a continuous real parameter but a discrete index d ∈ ℕ given by Bruhat-Tits tree depth, identified with p-adic valuation (t ↔ v_p(x)). Consequences: time is quantized at the Planck scale (tree root), temporal ordering is only partial (events on different branches are incomparable), and causality is tree-geodesic — A causes B iff B lies on the unique tree path from A to the root. This matters because it replaces the continuum of time with an ultrametric, non-Archimedean structure, potentially explaining Planck-scale discreteness and giving causality a purely combinatorial geometry. A paper could test it by: (1) deriving whether p-adic tree geodesics reproduce light-cone causal structure in a continuum limit; (2) simulating partial orders on Bruhat-Tits trees and checking recovery of standard temporal statistics; (3) formal analysis of whether unique-path causality avoids closed timelike curves and matches observed arrow-of-time constraints.
