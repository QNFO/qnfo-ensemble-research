## Research idea

Number Systems as Stratified Distinction Operations. The note conjectures that major number systems arise from successive primitive “distinction” operations: tallying gives ℕ, enclosure/comparison gives ℚ, convergent sequences give computable reals, lossy projection of a tree gives the continuum, phase enclosure gives ℂ, divisibility enclosure gives ℚ_p, and simultaneous valuations give the adeles. A paper could formalize this as a stratified hierarchy of closure or enclosure operators over a Laws-of-Form-like mark. For each stage, define the minimal algebraic structure generated by the operation, prove that it recovers the intended number system, and characterize the morphisms/information loss between adjacent strata—especially the “Monna-map” projection from a tree-like limit structure to a smooth continuum. Computational experiments could simulate measurement processes under each operator and compare their distinguishable states, precision, and loss profiles. The key testable claim is that the historical/structural progression ℕ → ℚ → ℝ → ℂ → ℚ_p → 𝔸 is not accidental but derivable from increasingly refined acts of distinction.

  For a positive integer $n$ let $H_n=\sum_{k=1}^{n}1/k$ be the $n$th harmonic number. In this note we prove that for any prime $p\ge 7$, $$ \sum_{k=1}^{p-1}\frac{H_k^2}{k^2} \equiv4/5pB_{p-5}\pmod{p^2}, $$ which confirms the conjecture recently proposed by Z. W. Sun. Furthermore, we also prove two similar congruences modulo $p^2$.
