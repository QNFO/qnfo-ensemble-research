## Research idea

Adelic ruler-independence forces Standard Model parameters to be rational. Conjecture: if physical law must be expressible consistently in every completion of the rationals (real and all p-adic places), then the Standard Model's free parameters cannot be arbitrary real numbers; they must be rationals or periods fixed by adelic product formulas. This would explain why couplings and masses look 'tuned': they are projections of a single adelic object, with the real value constrained by p-adic counterparts (as in adelic Veneziano amplitudes, where the string coupling becomes calculable). Test: formulate a toy sector (e.g., scalar propagator or simple amplitude) over Q, require well-definedness of the product over all places v, and derive whether consistency conditions (Artin product formula, anomaly-like constraints) pin the parameter to specific rational values. Simulate or formally check whether such constraints survive renormalization and whether the resulting rationals are compatible with observed mass/coupling ratios.
