## Research idea

P-adic Spacetime Evades Lorentz-Invariance No-Go Theorems. The author conjectures that no-go theorems forbidding locally finite, Poincaré-invariant discrete spacetime networks (proved over Archimedean Minkowski space) fail in a fundamentally p-adic, non-Archimedean reality. The proof's pillars—non-compact Lorentz boosts, continuous lightcones, uniform distributions on infinite hyperbolae, and Archimedean volume counting—do not translate to ultrametric geometry, where balls are clopen, symmetry groups are totally disconnected, and 'infinite distance' behaves differently. This matters because it suggests a route to discrete fundamental physics without observable preferred frames. A formal analysis could: (1) define a p-adic analogue of local finiteness and invariance under p-adic quadratic-form-preserving groups; (2) prove or refute an analogous no-go theorem over Q_p^n; (3) simulate hierarchical p-adic networks to test whether invariant locally finite graphs exist where real-valued ones cannot.
