## Research idea

Fine-Structure Constant as Adelic Cross-Ratio. The recurring conjecture across these threads is that the fine-structure constant α ≈ 1/137 is not a free parameter but a geometric/arithmetic invariant: expressible as a cross-ratio of rational points, a theta-function value on Farey arcs, or a p-adic/adelic product (Freund–Witten style Gamma-system zeta identities), with 'alpha as electron length ratio' as a scaling corollary. Why it matters: a derivation of α from number theory would constrain beyond-SM physics and unify real and p-adic descriptions. Test: formally attempt to reproduce α from known adelic string amplitudes and cross-ratio identities; numerically scan theta-constant and cross-ratio expressions at rational points for 1/137-level matches; check whether the proposed electron-length ratio reproduces α to experimental precision or fails, falsifying the claim.
