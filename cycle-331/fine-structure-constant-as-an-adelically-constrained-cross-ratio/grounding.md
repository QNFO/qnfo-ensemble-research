## Research idea

Fine-structure constant as an adelic cross ratio constrained by the product formula. The note conjectures that dimensionless physical constants, especially the fine-structure constant α, are cross ratios whose value is fixed (or tightly constrained) by adelic structure: the product formula |r|∞ ∏p |r|p = 1, p-adic beta functions, and the adelic Veneziano amplitude. The open question: can a derivation or computation extract α's observed value from adelic constraints alone, and why has this failed so far (boundary conditions only, no adelic action principle)? A paper could formalize this by (1) writing α as a cross ratio of adelic invariants and testing whether the product formula plus RG consistency yields a discrete spectrum containing 1/137; (2) simulating p-adic/hierarchical (Bethe-lattice) RG flows to see if ultrametric fixed points pin the coupling; (3) analyzing whether the Freund-Witten adelic product law imposes a quantization condition on couplings. Falsifiable: if no adelic constraint reproduces α within known running, the cross-ratio thesis is refuted as a determinative principle.
