## Research idea

Thoughts as quantum-informational Hamiltonians bridging physical and pre-geometric phases. Conjecture: cognition is not a physical process but a quantum-informational (Hamiltonian) state that couples a physical universe to a pre-geometric phase, with the Big Bang as a pre-geometric-to-geometric phase transition that 'froze' which thoughts can manifest. Testable angle: formalize a two-phase model where pre-geometric degrees of freedom are informational and a phase transition selects a Hamiltonian subspace; derive constraints on how biological 'quantum receptors' (microtubules, Posner molecules, magnetoreception-like structures) could maintain coherence and couple to that subspace. A computational study could model decoherence timescales and channel capacities of proposed receptors versus thermal noise, checking whether any physically plausible coupling survives, and whether such a framework yields predictions distinguishing it from standard neuroscience and quantum biology.
