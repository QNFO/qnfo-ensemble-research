## Research idea

Mechanism-first pre-registered testing of p-adic mass-ratio hypotheses. The author conjectures that any viable non-anthropocentric natural-units account of the particle mass spectrum must be grounded in a derivable mechanism — RG flow from a UV Yukawa boundary condition, anomaly-cancellation constraints, or moduli-stabilization symmetries — rather than numeric pattern-matching, and that such a mechanism yields falsifiable predictions of specific mass ratios or Compton-spectrum structure. Why it matters: prior adelic/p-adic approaches (j-invariant matches, cross-ratios on Bruhat–Tits trees, anharmonic-oscillator fits) produced only look-elsewhere false positives; a mechanism mandate converts numerology into testable physics. How to test: pick one mechanism (e.g., 2-loop RG running of SO(10) Yukawa unification), pre-register predicted IR mass ratios with locked statistical thresholds, generate independent surrogate spectra (not permutations of model output), and report the full null distribution to determine whether the mechanism beats the Standard Model null.
