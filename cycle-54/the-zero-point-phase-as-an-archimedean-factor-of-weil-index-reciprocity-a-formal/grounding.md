## Research idea

Zero-point phase as one factor of global Weil-index neutrality. Conjecture: the oscillator's Archimedean Maslov/vacuum phase e^{iπ/4} (zero-point ½) is not an Archimedean-exclusive structure but one local factor γ_∞ in the Weil-index product ∏_v γ_v = 1 over all places of ℚ, so the zero-point energy is adelically constrained by quadratic reciprocity. Why it matters: it would recast a foundational quantum constant as a reciprocity law, tying quantum vacuum structure to arithmetic and sharpening the thesis that only order (time, causality) is genuinely Archimedean. Test: formally verify that the metaplectic Weil indices satisfy reciprocity for the quadratic form x² and compute the p-adic factors γ_p; then simulate/derive whether imposing ∏_v γ_v = 1 constrains admissible vacuum phases or spectra (e.g., Dragovich-style adelic oscillator eigenstates), and state a disconfirmation condition: if Archimedean Maslov phase can be varied independently of p-adic data without breaking self-duality, the conjecture fails.
