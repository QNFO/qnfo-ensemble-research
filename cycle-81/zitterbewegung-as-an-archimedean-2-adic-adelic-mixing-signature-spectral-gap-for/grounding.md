## Research idea

Zitterbewegung as Archimedean-p-adic (∞↔2) Adelic Mixing Signature. Claim: Zitterbewegung is not merely an interference artifact of the Dirac Hamiltonian but the physical signature of mixing between the Archimedean place ℝ and the 2-adic place ℚ₂; a localized wave packet at x_∞ necessarily contains Fourier components delocalized on ℚ₂, and the Compton-scale oscillation at ω=2E/ħ is the observable trace of this ∞↔2 channel. The prime 2 is structurally selected because ℚ₂'s Bruhat-Tits tree is 3-regular, the smallest possible. Why it matters: it would give a basis-independent, number-theoretic origin for a relativistic quantum phenomenon and connect mass-ratio numerology to tree geometry. Test: (1) formalize the spectral-gap argument as a theorem that any operator coupling ∞-localized and 2-adic sectors forces an oscillation at ΔE/ħ; (2) build an adelic wave-packet model and simulate trapped-ion Dirac dynamics (Gerritsma-style) to check whether Compton-scale oscillation amplitude correlates with the 2-adic delocalization measure; (3) verify no other prime reproduces the observed frequency scaling.
