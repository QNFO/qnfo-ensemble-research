## Research idea

Test the hypothesis that a universal discrete scaling symmetry with factor 2 governs fundamental physics across unrelated domains. Claim: if the universe is fundamentally invariant under f ~ 2f (halving/doubling), then log-periodic signatures with the same factor should appear in both few-body quantum spectra (Efimov physics) and particle mass hierarchies or other fundamental observables. Current evidence counts against this: Efimov towers have coupling-dependent scaling factors (e^2π/s_0, not 2), and no universal factor-2 log-periodicity has been observed in cosmological or particle data. However, discrete scale invariance (DSI) with log-periodic modulation is confirmed in Efimov physics, Dirac materials, and RG limit cycles — suggesting the broader intuition (discrete scaling loops replacing the continuum) has empirical support, but the specific factor-2 universality does not.
