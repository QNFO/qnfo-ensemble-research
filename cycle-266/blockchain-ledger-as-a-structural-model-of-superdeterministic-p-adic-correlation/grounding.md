## Research idea

Blockchain Ledger as Structural Model of Superdeterministic p-adic Correlations. Conjecture: a superdeterministic universe with p-adic (non-archimedean, hierarchical) correlations is structurally isomorphic to a blockchain ledger, where each event-block's state is a deterministic hash of prior history, and p-adic distance measures shared causal prefix. This matters because it could give superdeterminism a concrete mathematical formalism: global correlations (violating statistical independence in Bell tests) arise exactly as blockchain immutability arises from chained hashes, while p-adic tree structure captures fork-like branching of possible histories. Test by: (1) formally defining a category or graph isomorphism between p-adic integer chains and hash-linked ledgers; (2) simulating Bell-type experiments on a p-adic-correlated deterministic model and checking whether correlations reproduce quantum statistics; (3) proving whether the non-archimedean metric's prefix-agreement property implies the no-signaling constraints superdeterminism must satisfy.
