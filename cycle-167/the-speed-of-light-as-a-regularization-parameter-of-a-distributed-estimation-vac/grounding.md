## Research idea

Speed of Light as Vacuum's Regularization Parameter. The note conjectures that fundamental constants—especially c—function as regularization parameters of an 'active, adversarial' quantum substrate: finite causal speed acts as an early-stopping rule that prevents the vacuum's distributed estimation network from 'overfitting' high-frequency quantum fluctuations, so only low-frequency stable signals (matter, energy) propagate as reality; gravity is reframed as latency congestion in this bandwidth-limited channel. A paper could formalize this by modeling the vacuum as a distributed network estimating an unstable stochastic process under Khan–Jadbabaie network tracing capacity bounds, deriving whether a finite propagation-speed constraint provably suppresses high-frequency divergence (analogous to Raskutti–Wainwright–Yu early-stopping guarantees), and simulating lattice field or agent-based models where varying the 'signal velocity' tests whether stable macroscopic structures emerge only within a critical bandwidth window, and whether 'congestion' reproduces curvature-like effects.
