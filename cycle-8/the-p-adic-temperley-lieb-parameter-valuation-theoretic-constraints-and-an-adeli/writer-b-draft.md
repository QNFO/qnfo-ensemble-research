# The p-Adic Temperley-Lieb Parameter: Valuation-Theoretic Constraints on a Claimed Predictive Framework

## Abstract

The claim that a "p-adic Temperley-Lieb parameter" predicts quantum system behavior with at least 95% accuracy is, as stated, unfalsifiable: it specifies no systems, no metric, and no error model. This paper does not defend the claim; it disciplines it. We construct the only mathematically canonical candidate for such a parameter, δ_p = p + p⁻¹ ∈ Q_p, arising from the Temperley-Lieb (TL) relation e_i² = δ e_i, and we analyze it with explicit p-adic valuation arithmetic. We prove that the quantum dimension of the rank-2 Jones-Wenzl projector, d_2 = (p⁴ + p² + 1)/p², has p-adic valuation exactly −2 for every prime p, so it is never a p-adic integer: the projector exists over Q_p but never over Z_p. We compute d_2 and d_3 for p = 2, 3, 5, 7 explicitly and show that nothing distinguishes p = 3, despite the known p = 3 phase-transition singularity in the p-adic Potts model. Our results yield one sharp, falsifiable prediction (universal non-integrality) and demonstrate that the 95% accuracy claim cannot survive contact with the algebra. The honest conclusion is negative for the strong claim but constructive for a weaker, testable program.

## 1. Introduction

Two mathematical threads have largely developed in isolation. The first is the Temperley-Lieb algebra, a diagrammatic algebra generated by elements e_1, …, e_{n−1} satisfying e_i² = δ e_i, e_i e_j = e_j e_i for |i−j| > 1, and e_i e_{i±1} e_i = e_i, where δ (the loop parameter) is a scalar. This algebra underlies the Jones polynomial, anyonic quantum computation, and lattice Potts models. The second thread is p-adic mathematical physics, in which the field of p-adic numbers Q_p (numbers written in base p extending infinitely to the left, with distance |x|_p = p^{−v_p(x)}, where v_p(x) is the exponent of p in the factorization of x) replaces or complements the reals.

The research idea under examination asserts that a "p-adic Temperley-Lieb parameter" predicts quantum behavior with ≥95% accuracy. This paper treats the idea as a hypothesis to be formalized and stress-tested. Our contributions are: (i) a canonical construction of the parameter; (ii) explicit valuation computations showing a universal structural feature (non-integrality of quantum dimensions); (iii) a demonstration that the celebrated p = 3 singularity of p-adic Potts models does not appear in the TL parameter; and (iv) an assessment of what, if anything, could ground an accuracy claim.

## 2. Background and Related Work

The Temperley-Lieb algebra itself has a rich structural literature relevant to any p-adic extension. Eastham [1] defines a tower of affine Temperley-Lieb algebras of type Ã_n and Markov elements therein, proving that any trace on the type Ã_2 algebra is uniquely determined by its values on Markov elements; this matters because trace uniqueness is what would license evaluating any putative p-adic invariant on a finite generating set, making computation finite. The basis question is addressed by work using rewriting systems [2], which defines TL both as a presented algebra and diagrammatically, searches algorithmically for a basis via rewriting theory, and generalizes to an oriented version requiring categorical machinery; a p-adic extension inherits the same presentation, so normal-form algorithms transfer verbatim, but the oriented generalization shows the presentation alone underdetermines the "right" category. Kauffman's teleportation analysis [3] realizes braid teleportation configurations and virtual braid representations within standard teleportation, devising diagrammatic rules for circuits with maximally entangled states; this is the strongest existing bridge between TL algebra and actual quantum information processing, and any predictive claim about "quantum systems" must ultimately route through such operational encodings. The homological sensitivity of TL to its parameter is exposed by the Anick resolution construction for TL_3 [4], which shows that the scalar τ in the defining relation enters the differentials of both the free resolution and the bar homology Tor_*^{TL_3}(C, C), implying homology depends on the arithmetic nature of τ — generic versus root-of-unity — a dependence we exploit in Section 4.

On the p-adic side, the Potts model on a Cayley tree [5] reduces p-adic Gibbs measures to a recursive equation and proves a phase transition occurs if and only if p = 3, for any nonzero interaction; this is the sharpest known "p = 3 is special" result and the natural benchmark against which any p-adic TL parameter must be compared. Adelic quantum mechanics [6] formulates wave functions of p-adic and adelic arguments with complex values, establishing the framework in which a p-adic parameter could act on physical states. The p-adic wavelet transform [7] proposes a tool for hierarchic quantum systems, suggesting the natural function spaces on which a p-adic TL action would live. Adelic cosmology [8] introduces p-adic worlds of p-adic matter composing the universe alongside ordinary matter, illustrating the maximal (and, we argue, untestable) reading of p-adic physics.

The QNFO corpus frames the speculative context: topological aliasing and holographic readout [9] and the Bruhat-Tits tree as unifying geometric object [10] propose non-Archimedean geometry as a readout substrate; the red-team assessment of the Harmonic Paradigm [11] explicitly criticizes a program that invoked Ostrowski's theorem and p-adic structures while its core mechanisms remained disconnected from them — a caution this paper takes seriously; and the adelic cross-domain program [12] claims the semigroup {2^a·3^b·5^c} simultaneously realizes the Standard Model mass spectrum, a GKP code lattice, the Efimov discretuum, and a diagonal Bruhat-Tits embedding, with a 15-entry calibration register — the kind of multi-domain numerology that a single-parameter accuracy claim must be distinguished from. We cite all twelve works because the bibliography is the complete permitted literature; this is a limitation discussed in Section 6.

## 3. Methods

**Definition of the parameter.** The TL algebra TL_n(δ) over a commutative ring is generated by e_1,…,e_{n−1} with relations e_i² = δ e_i, e_i e_{i±1} e_i = e_i, and far commutativity. The loop parameter δ admits the canonical factorization δ = z + z⁻¹. The unique p-adic-canonical choice is z = p, giving

δ_p = p + p⁻¹ ∈ Q_p.

This is canonical because p is the uniformizer of Q_p (the element defining the valuation), so δ_p is the TL parameter naturally adapted to the p-adic norm, exactly as δ = q + q⁻¹ with q = e^{iθ} is adapted to the unit circle in the real/complex theory.

**Tools.** (a) The p-adic valuation v_p: for x = p^k·(a/b) with p ∤ ab, v_p(x) = k and |x|_p = p^{−k}; x is a p-adic integer iff v_p(x) ≥ 0. (b) The Jones-Wenzl projector f_n, the unique element with e_i f_n = f_n e_i = 0 and f_n² = f_n, which exists iff the quantum dimensions d_1,…,d_{n−1} are invertible, where d_n satisfies d_0 = 1, d_1 = δ, d_{n+1} = δ d_n − d_{n−1}. (c) The genericity criterion from [4]: bar homology of TL_3(τ) depends on whether τ is a root of unity.

**Methodological stance.** We do not simulate anything. Every number below is derived by exact rational arithmetic with stated inputs.

## 4. Analysis

**Input numbers and sources.** The prime inputs p = 2, 3, 5, 7 are the first four primes (standard). The defining relation e_i² = δ e_i and the projector existence criterion are standard TL theory (cf. [2], [3]). The recursion d_{n+1} = δ d_n − d_{n−1} with d_0 = 1, d_1 = δ is the standard quantum dimension recursion. The homological genericity criterion is from [4]. The p = 3 phase-transition benchmark is from [5].

**Step 1: δ_p for each prime.**

- p = 2: δ_2 = 2 + 1/2 = 5/2. v_2(5/2) = −1, |δ_2|_2 = 2.
- p = 3: δ_3 = 3 + 1/3 = 10/3. v_3(10/3) = −1, |δ_3|_3 = 3.
- p = 5: δ_5 = 5 + 1/5 = 26/5. v_5(26/5) = −1, |δ_5|_5 = 5.
- p = 7: δ_7 = 7 + 1/7 = 50/7. v_7(50/7) = −1, |δ_7|_7 = 7.

In general v_p(δ_p) = −1 for every prime, since p² + 1 ≡ 1 (mod p) is not divisible by p.

**Step 2: quantum dimension d_2 = δ² − 1.**

d_2 = δ_p² − 1 = (p + p⁻¹)² − 1 = p² + 2 + p⁻² − 1 = p² + 1 + p⁻² = (p⁴ + p² + 1)/p².

- p = 2: p⁴ + p² + 1 = 16 + 4 + 1 = 21, so d_2 = 21/4. v_2(21/4) = 0 − 2 = −2. |d_2|_2 = 4.
- p = 3: 81 + 9 + 1 = 91, d_2 = 91/9. 91 = 7·13, not divisible by 3, so v_3(91/9) = −2. |d_2|_3 = 9.
- p = 5: 625 + 25 + 1 = 651 = 3·7·31, not divisible by 5, so d_2 = 651/25, v_5 = −2. |d_2|_5 = 25.
- p = 7: 2401 + 49 + 1 = 2451 = 3·19·43, not divisible by 7, so d_2 = 2451/49, v_7 = −2. |d_2|_7 = 49.

**General claim and proof.** For any prime p, p⁴ + p² + 1 ≡ 1 (mod p), so p ∤ (p⁴ + p² + 1), hence v_p(d_2) = −2 exactly. Therefore d_2 ∉ Z_p for every prime p: the rank-2 Jones-Wenzl projector exists over Q_p (d_2 ≠ 0) but is never integral over Z_p. Its leading term is always p⁻² times a p-adic unit.

**Step 3: quantum dimension d_3 = δ³ − 2δ.**

d_3 = δ_p³ − 2δ_p = δ_p(δ_p² − 2) = (p + p⁻¹)(p² + p⁻²) = p³ + p + p⁻¹ + p⁻³ = (p⁶ + p⁴ + p² + 1)/p³.

- p = 2: 64 + 16 + 4 + 1 = 85, d_3 = 85/8. 85 odd, so v_2 = −3. |d_3|_2 = 8.
- p = 3: 729 + 81 + 9 + 1 = 820 = 2²·5·41, not divisible by 3, so d_3 = 820/27, v_3 = −3. |d_3|_3 = 27.
- p = 5: 15625 + 625 + 25 + 1 = 16276 = 2²·7·7·83, not divisible by 5, so d_3 = 16276/125, v_5 = −3.
- p = 7: 117649 + 2401 + 49 + 1 = 120100 = 2²·5²·1201, not divisible by 7, so d_3 = 120100/343, v_7 = −3.

Again v_p(d_3) = −3 universally, since p⁶ + p⁴ + p² + 1 ≡ 1 (mod p). Pattern: v_p(d_n) = −n for all n ≥ 1.

**Step 4: the p = 3 test.** Reference [5] proves the p-adic Potts model on the Cayley tree has a phase transition iff p = 3. If the p-adic TL parameter carried physical predictive content in the Potts channel, one would expect a singularity at p = 3. But v_3(d_2) = −2 and v_3(d_3) = −3 are structurally identical to p = 2, 5, 7: δ_3 = 10/3 is not a root of unity in Q_3 (its reduction 10/3 = 3·(10/9); 10/9 reduces to the unit 10·9⁻¹ mod 3 ≡ 1·0⁻¹ — more directly, δ_3 ∈ Q is rational and the only rational roots of unity are ±1, and 10/3 ∉ {1, −1}). Hence by the criterion of [4], the bar homology of TL_3(δ_3) is the generic case, as it is for every prime. No p = 3 anomaly appears in the TL parameter.

**Step 5: what would "95% accuracy" even mean?** Suppose accuracy is measured as agreement of a predicted observable with a measured value within tolerance ε. Then a parameter taking a fixed value δ_p per prime makes at most |{p : prediction matches}|/|{p tested}| correct predictions. Since our analysis shows the parameter's algebraic behavior is uniform in p (same valuations, same genericity class), any accuracy statistic over primes would be either 0% or 100% on structurally determined questions — never an interesting 95%. The 95% figure has no derivation.

## 5. Results

All numbers below were computed in Section 4 by exact arithmetic; none are simulated or measured.

1. **δ_p values:** δ_2 = 5/2, δ_3 = 10/3, δ_5 = 26/5, δ_7 = 50/7, each with v_p = −1.
2. **Universal non-integrality (theorem):** v_p(d_2) = −2 for all primes p, proved via p⁴ + p² + 1 ≡ 1 (mod p). Explicit instances: d_2 = 21/4 (p=2), 91/9 (p=3), 651/25 (p=5), 2451/49 (p=7).
3. **Rank-3 dimensions:** d_3 = 85/8 (p=2), 820/27 (p=3), 16276/125 (p=5), 120100/343 (p=7), with v_p(d_3) = −3 universally.
4. **No p = 3 anomaly:** the TL parameter at p = 3 is generic (δ_3 = 10/3 is rational and not ±1, hence not a root of unity), so by [4] the TL_3 bar homology is the generic case; the Potts phase transition of [5] is invisible at the level of δ_p.
5. **Projection (clearly labeled):** if one defines "predictive accuracy" as the fraction of primes p ≤ N for which the p-adic TL parameter yields a distinct algebraic prediction, our theorem implies this fraction is 0 for all N, with zero uncertainty, since the valuation pattern v_p(d_n) = −n is proved for all primes. This is a projection from a proved theorem, not an empirical measurement.
6. **The 95% claim:** no computation in this paper supports it, and the structural uniformity result (item 5) shows a single scalar parameter cannot generate a 95%-style partial success rate on valuation-determined questions.

## 6. Discussion

**Limitations.** First, the bibliography is small (12 works) and we cite all of it; the p-adic TL intersection is genuinely thin, and our literature grounding for the physical side rests mainly on [5]–[8], none of which uses TL parameters. Second, our choice δ_p = p + p⁻¹ is canonical but not unique; alternatives such as δ = q + q⁻¹ with q a p-adic root of unity would change every computation, though such q exists in Q_p only when p ≡ 1 (mod order), itself a prime-dependent constraint worth future study. Third, we analyzed only n ≤ 3 projectors; the pattern v_p(d_n) = −n is proved for n = 2, 3 and conjectured beyond.

**Failure modes.** The universal non-integrality result could fail if the projector existence criterion is applied over a coefficient ring where d_2 is invertible despite negative valuation — over Q_p it is invertible, so the projector exists; our claim is only about integrality over Z_p, and conflating these would be an error. The "no p = 3 anomaly" conclusion could be an artifact of looking only at valuations; finer invariants (e.g., traces on affine TL of type Ã_2 per [1], evaluated p-adically) might yet see p = 3.

**What would falsify our claims.** (i) Exhibit a prime p with p | (p⁴ + p² + 1) — impossible by the congruence, so this claim is proved, not merely tested. (ii) Exhibit a physical observable, defined operationally as in [3], whose measured values across a stated system set match δ_p-derived predictions at a rate statistically distinguishable from chance — this would falsify our negative assessment of the 95% claim. (iii) Show that the Potts p = 3 transition of [5] propagates into TL traces, falsifying item 4.

**Arguing against ourselves.** The strongest counterargument: physical quantum systems are not indexed by primes, so asking δ_p to "predict behavior" may be a category error, and our demolition of the 95% figure attacks a strawman. We accept this partially — but the idea as posed makes the accuracy claim, and the burden is on it. Conversely, our non-integrality theorem is a genuinely new structural fact: it says any p-adic TL-based topological quantum computation scheme (in the spirit of [3]) working over Z_p coefficients is impossible with the canonical parameter, forcing denominators and hence error-control questions. That is a constructive, testable consequence.

**Open questions.** Does v_p(d_n) = −n hold for all n (provable by induction from the recursion and the congruence structure)? Do Markov-trace values on Ã_2 [1] detect p = 3? Can the wavelet basis of [7] carry a TL module action with δ_p, and does the resulting spectral data match any hierarchic quantum system?

## 7. Conclusion

We formalized the p-adic Temperley-Lieb parameter as δ_p = p + p⁻¹ and proved, by explicit valuation arithmetic, that its quantum dimensions are never p-adic integral (v_p(d_2) = −2, v_p(d_3) = −3, universally), that it exhibits no p = 3 anomaly despite the Potts-model precedent, and that it is homologically generic for every prime. The claimed 95% predictive accuracy is unsupported and, on valuation-determined questions, structurally unattainable as a partial success rate. What survives is a precise, falsifiable algebraic program: p-adic TL theory with canonical parameter forces non-integral projectors, a constraint any future physical application must confront.
