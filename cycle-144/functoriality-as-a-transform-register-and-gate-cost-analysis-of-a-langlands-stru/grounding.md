## Research idea

Adelic quantum computers implementing Langlands functoriality via p-adic transforms. Conjecture: the Langlands correspondence between Galois representations and automorphic forms admits a computational realization in which p-adic Fourier transforms on an 'adelic quantum computer'—a hybrid architecture with native registers over both archimedean and non-archimedean completions—implement Langlands functoriality at the algorithmic level. Why it matters: it would recast modularity (e.g., matching L-functions of elliptic curves and modular forms) as a quantum-computable map, potentially yielding new algorithms for L-function evaluation and factorization via Lenstra-style p-adic point counting. Testable path: (1) formalize a finite/adelic toy model where the correspondence is exactly computable (e.g., GL(2) over function fields or finite fields), (2) derive the unitary operator implementing the local Langlands correspondence at a fixed prime, (3) simulate its composition across primes to verify it reproduces known Hecke-eigenvalue matches, and (4) analyze query/complexity costs of the resulting functoriality transform versus classical modular-form computations.
