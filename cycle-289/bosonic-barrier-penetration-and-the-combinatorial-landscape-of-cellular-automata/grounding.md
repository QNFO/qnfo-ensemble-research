## Research idea

Bosonic Barrier Penetration as Mechanism for UV Cellular Communication. The author conjectures that because bosons like photons are exempt from the Pauli exclusion principle—the very mechanism that makes matter 'impenetrable'—UV photons can traverse biological barriers that block fermionic matter, and that cells exploit this for intercellular communication and transport. A paper could test this by modeling UV photon propagation through cellular membranes and cytoplasmic tissue (absorption, scattering, tunneling-like transmission probabilities via Maxwell/Schrödinger wave equations), comparing predicted penetration depths with reported observations of UV-mediated cell signaling, and quantifying whether bosonic statistics confer a measurable transmission advantage over fermionic matter transport in the same wavelength regime.
