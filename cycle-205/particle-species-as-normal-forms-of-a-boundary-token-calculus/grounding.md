## Research idea

Particle Species as Normal Forms of a Boundary Token Calculus. The author conjectures that fundamental particles correspond to irreducible normal forms of a syntactic token calculus with three reduction rules (Calling, Crossing, Void): stable particles are normal forms, interactions are local reductions of juxtaposed expressions, fermionic antisymmetry arises from Crossing-generated self-canceling nesting, bosonic symmetry from Calling idempotence, mass from nesting depth, confinement from deep locked composites, and the graviton's form reduces to the void, explaining non-renormalizability of quantum gravity. This matters because it would derive particle statistics, mass hierarchies, and interaction vertices from pure syntax. A formal analysis could define the rewrite system precisely, prove confluence/termination, enumerate all normal forms up to bounded depth, and test whether the resulting classification reproduces the Standard Model's particle spectrum, exchange statistics, and allowed interaction vertices; simulations of random expression reduction could check claimed decay channels like beta decay and pair production.
