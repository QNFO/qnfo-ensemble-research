## Research idea

Algebraic Origin of Zitterbewegung Oscillation. The note conjectures a hierarchy of mathematical 'ownership' of zitterbewegung: oscillation is fundamentally an algebraic/spectral fact (interference between positive- and negative-energy eigenspaces separated by 2mc², forced by the anticommutator {H,α_j} = 2cp_j), while calculus merely produces the solution, trigonometry is notation, and geometry is only a visualizable shadow. Testable claim: the oscillation arises whenever a velocity operator fails to commute with the Hamiltonian due to Clifford-algebraic structure, independent of dimension or representation. A formal analysis could generalize the exact solution α_j(t) = cp_jH⁻¹ + Z_j e^{-2iHt/ħ} to arbitrary Clifford algebras and curved backgrounds, proving that oscillation frequency always equals an inter-eigenspace beat frequency (E₊−E₋)/ħ. Simulations of Dirac wave-packet dynamics (e.g., graphene or trapped-ion analogues) could verify that suppressing negative-energy components eliminates zitterbewegung, confirming the algebraic-over-geometric origin.
