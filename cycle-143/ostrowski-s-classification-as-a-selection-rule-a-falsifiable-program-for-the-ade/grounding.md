## Research idea

Standard Model gauge group as real-place shadow of adelic symmetry. Conjecture: since Ostrowski's theorem forces exactly two completions of Q (R and Q_p), the full symmetry group of physics should be adelic, with the observed gauge group SU(3)xSU(2)xU(1) being only the Archimedean 'shadow' of a product structure over all places of Q. Testable consequence: coupling constants and particle representations should satisfy adelic consistency conditions. A theoretical program could (1) attempt to realize the Standard Model as a local component of an automorphic representation on A_Q, (2) derive constraints on gauge couplings from adelic product formulas or L-function functional equations, and (3) check whether Bruhat-Tits tree dynamics at p-adic places reproduce a frequency-space sector compatible with real-place spacetime physics. Success would give a unification route requiring no new symmetries beyond Ostrowski's classification; failure to embed the gauge group adelicly would falsify the conjecture internally.
