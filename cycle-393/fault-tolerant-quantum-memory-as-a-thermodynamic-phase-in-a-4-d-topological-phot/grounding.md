## Research idea

Fault Tolerance as Thermodynamic Phase via 4D Topological Bandgap. Claim: quantum error correction can be replaced by hardware-intrinsic protection—physically instantiating a 4D Harper-Hofstadter Hamiltonian in a silicon-nitride/lithium-niobate photonic lattice driven by a THz Floquet field, so that a topological mass gap (~14.5 meV) exceeds the thermal energy (kBT ≈ 6.9 meV at 80 K) and errors become Boltzmann-suppressed high-energy excitations rather than algorithmically corrected events. This reframes fault tolerance as a thermodynamic property of a material phase, eliminating the superlinear energetic cost of active syndrome decoding. Testable: (1) derive an effective Floquet Hamiltonian for frequency/OAM synthetic dimensions and verify a nonzero second Chern number and gap stability against realistic drive-induced heating; (2) simulate a Lindblad/open-system model comparing logical error rates and energy budgets of this passive scheme versus surface-code decoding at scale; (3) analyze whether thermo-optic detuning can be suppressed by ballistic phonon extraction fast enough to keep the gap open in a non-equilibrium steady state.
