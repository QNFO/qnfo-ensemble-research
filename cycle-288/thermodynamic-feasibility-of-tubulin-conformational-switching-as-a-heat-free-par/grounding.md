## Research idea

Tubulin Conformational Switching as Heat-Free Parallel Computation. The note conjectures that microtubules act as parallel processors whose tubulin dimers switch between two conformational states like transistors, with computation carried by shape changes rather than electron flow, yielding near-zero heat dissipation and enabling dense in-cell information processing. A paper could test this by (1) deriving an energy/heat budget for conformational switching from tubulin's known mechanical properties and comparing it to Landauer-limited electronic switching at equivalent density; (2) simulating state propagation on the microtubule lattice to determine whether dimer geometry supports computationally useful dynamics (e.g., cellular automata or resonant wave patterns); and (3) checking whether switching rates and coherence times are physically plausible. This would quantify whether the analogy is thermodynamically and dynamically viable or merely metaphorical.
