## Research idea

Can finite-group TQFTs yield continuous Standard Model gauge symmetry?. The note argues that a Walker-Wang model built from Z(Rep(SL(2,3))) ⊠ SPT_3 cannot host the Standard Model because finite-group Drinfeld centers have finitely many simple objects and no local continuous gauge symmetry, while the SM requires SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1). The implicit research question: can any finite, rank-N braided fusion category (or a sequence of such categories) approximate or dynamically generate continuous Lie-group gauge symmetry with chiral fermions and anomaly cancellation, or is there a no-go theorem? This matters because it would rigorously confirm or refute a whole class of 'topological genesis of the Standard Model' proposals. A formal analysis could proceed by (1) proving that any lattice TQFT with finite simple-object set admits only finite gauge symmetry in the continuum limit, or (2) constructing explicit renormalization-group/coarse-graining limits of Walker-Wang models and checking whether SU(N) loop charges emerge, and (3) testing anomaly matching for any candidate chiral anyon content.
