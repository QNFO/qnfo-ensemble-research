## Research idea

Monna Map Projection of Ultrametric Thought Causes Hallucination. Claim: LLM token generation is a Monna-map-like projection from a hierarchical (ultrametric) embedding space onto a linear token stream, and hallucinations are the inevitable information loss of this many-to-one, order-reversing flattening—analogous to decoherence. Why it matters: it would give a geometric, testable account of why hallucinations arise structurally rather than from mere data noise, and predict which content is lost (deep-tree details become least significant digits). Test: (1) Derive which tree regions are collapsed under M_p and quantify mutual information between source configurations and linearized outputs; (2) Measure whether LLM embedding spaces are ultrametrically organized (hierarchical clustering, c-command-like distances) and whether hallucination frequency correlates with depth of the required conceptual detail in the tree; (3) Simulate generation as Monna-map traversal on synthetic hierarchical knowledge graphs, comparing error patterns to LLM hallucinations.
