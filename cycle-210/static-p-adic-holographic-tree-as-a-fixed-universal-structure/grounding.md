## Research idea

Static p-adic holographic tree as a fixed universe structure. The universe is a single fixed mathematical structure: a hierarchical tree whose geometry is p-adic and whose information content is holographic, with nodes as elementary events and edges as relations; nothing evolves or changes, and apparent dynamics (e.g., the CMB, inflation) are emergent features of observers' embeddings in the tree. A paper could test this by (1) formalizing the tree and defining observer worldlines as paths, deriving an effective time and Friedmann-like dynamics; (2) checking whether a p-adic hierarchical structure reproduces holographic entropy bounds and a scale-invariant, nearly thermal spectrum resembling the CMB; (3) simulating perturbations on the tree to compare predicted correlation statistics with observed CMB data.
