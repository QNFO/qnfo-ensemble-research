## Research idea

Wheeler-DeWitt zero-mode as a Majorana-type topological zero mode. The author conjectures that the Wheeler-DeWitt Hamiltonian constraint's zero-mode (HΨ=0) is structurally analogous to a Majorana zero mode: a topologically protected, non-oscillating eigenstate at zero energy, implying a fundamentally stationary universe and suggesting zero-mode physics bridges quantum gravity and topological quantum computing. A paper could test this by (1) formally comparing the Wheeler-DeWitt constraint operator's spectral properties in minisuperspace models to the BdG Hamiltonian hosting Majorana modes, checking for shared features like particle-hole (self-conjugate) symmetry and index theorems; (2) deriving whether minisuperspace zero-modes exhibit topological protection under deformations of the lapse/curvature terms; and (3) simulating whether such protection yields decoherence-free states relevant to qubit design. Confirming the analogy would give quantum gravity an operational, computational signature; refuting it would clarify that the shared 'zero-mode' label is purely nominal.
