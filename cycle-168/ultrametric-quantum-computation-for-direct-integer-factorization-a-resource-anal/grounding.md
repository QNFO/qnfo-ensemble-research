## Research idea

Ultrametric Quantum Computer for Direct Factorization. The author conjectures that an 'adelic quantum computer'—one whose native Hilbert structure is ultrametric (p-adic) rather than Archimedean—could access number-theoretic structure (factorization) directly, bypassing the phase-estimation detour in Shor's algorithm. Superposition is claimed to be an artifact of projecting a discrete, tree-based ontology onto a continuous measurement screen, with the adelic product formula enforcing the global picture. This matters because it suggests a fundamentally different computational model for factoring with potentially different complexity characteristics. A paper could test this by (1) formally defining an ultrametric quantum computing model with p-adic Fourier transforms replacing the QFT, (2) proving or disproving that period-finding over Z/N admits a p-adic formulation without eigenvalue estimation, and (3) simulating small instances to compare resource requirements against Shor's approach.
