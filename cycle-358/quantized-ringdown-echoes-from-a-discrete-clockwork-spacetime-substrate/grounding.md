## Research idea

Quantized Ringdown Echoes from Discrete Clockwork Spacetime. Claim: if spacetime is generated by discrete computational steps on a topological substrate rather than a continuous manifold, black hole ringdowns should not be smooth chirps but should contain quantized spectral lines—faint, regularly spaced 'teeth' in the frequency spectrum after the merger peak. This is a sharp, falsifiable discriminator between continuous GR and a discrete/clockwork substrate model, testable with existing LIGO/Virgo data and next-generation detectors (LISA, Cosmic Explorer). A paper could investigate: (1) derive the predicted line spacing from the model's winding-number/phase-step dynamics and compute expected amplitudes relative to detector noise; (2) run matched-filter and spectral analyses on published ringdown events (e.g., GW150914) to bound any such comb-like structure; (3) formalize whether a discrete-time substrate can reproduce the continuous quasinormal-mode spectrum in the low-frequency limit, checking consistency with Jacobson-style thermodynamic emergence of Einstein's equations.
