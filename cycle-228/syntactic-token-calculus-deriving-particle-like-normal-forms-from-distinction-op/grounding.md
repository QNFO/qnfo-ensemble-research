## Research idea

Syntactic Token Calculus: Particles as Stable Normal Forms. The author conjectures that a two-primitive calculus (a mark '#' and nested enclosures '[ ]', with local reduction/crossing rules) generates expressions whose irreducible normal forms can be identified with elementary particles, with nesting depth mapping to a Bruhat-Tits tree whose ultrametric distance encodes distinctions. This matters because it would derive particle-like stable structures purely from distinction-drawing (Spencer-Brown style), without assuming physical fields. A formal analysis could: (1) define the reduction system precisely and prove confluence/termination of normal forms; (2) enumerate all irreducible expressions up to depth n and classify them; (3) test whether the resulting classes exhibit conserved-quantity-like invariants (e.g., parity under enclosure depth) resembling particle quantum numbers; (4) simulate rewriting dynamics to check which normal forms are dynamically stable attractors, comparing the count and structure to known particle multiplets.
