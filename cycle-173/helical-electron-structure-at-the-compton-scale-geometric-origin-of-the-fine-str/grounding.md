## Research idea

Helical electron structure at Compton scale with pitch ratio alpha. The electron is not point-like but has internal helical structure at the Compton scale (~2.4e-12 m), with a null-curve pitch-to-circumference ratio equal to the fine-structure constant alpha ≈ 1/137. This matters because it would explain alpha geometrically and predict observable deviations from point-particle QED. A theoretical paper could compute the electron's electromagnetic form factors from the space-curve geometry of the helix, derive calculable corrections to the QED vertex, and compare the predicted anomalous magnetic moment (g-2) with the exquisitely measured value. It could also predict a parity-violating asymmetry in polarized scattering from the helix's intrinsic torsion, and diffraction-like resonances in e+e- scattering at momentum transfers ~1/lambda_c. Falsifiability: if no structure appears below lambda_c and g-2 matches point-particle QED exactly, the literal helical model is ruled out.
