P-adic ultrametric calculus as the base layer of quantum dynamics. Claim: the real number line should be replaced by p-adic fields as the foundational arena of quantum reality, with dynamics governed by non-local fractional derivatives (e.g., Vladimirov operators) and Haar-measure integrals instead of ordinary calculus. Why it matters: p-adic topology is totally disconnected and ultrametric, potentially encoding discrete/fractal Planck-scale structure and explaining quantum non-locality, while real spacetime emerges only as an approximation. Test: derive a p-adic Schrödinger/heat equation using Vladimirov pseudodifferential operators, simulate its evolution on hierarchical trees, and check whether real-limit or ultrametric spectral signatures (e.g., energy spectra, path-integral amplitudes as in p-adic string theory) reproduce known quantum predictions or yield falsifiable deviations.
