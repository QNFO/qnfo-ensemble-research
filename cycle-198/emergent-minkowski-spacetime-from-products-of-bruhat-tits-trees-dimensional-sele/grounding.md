## Research idea

Emergent Minkowski spacetime and string critical dimensions from Bruhat–Tits tree forests. Conjecture: a product of D Bruhat–Tits trees, with one tree's directed edges supplying a causal order, yields an emergent (D−1,1)-dimensional Minkowski spacetime in a continuum/adelic limit (p→∞ or prime mixing), and consistency conditions (anomaly cancellation, supersymmetric continuum limit, automorphism-group gauge symmetries) single out D=10 or 11, matching string/M-theory critical dimensions, with extra trees acting as compactified dimensions. Why it matters: it would derive spacetime dimensionality and Lorentzian signature from purely combinatorial, non-Archimedean data, giving a background-independent lattice for string theory. Test: formally construct the product boundary ∂F = ∏ P¹(Q_{p_i}), prove or disprove that a scaling limit recovers a Lorentzian metric; simulate p-adic string amplitudes (adelic Veneziano integrals) on the forest and check whether anomaly/supersymmetry constraints force specific D; analyze whether automorphism groups of compact-tree products yield root lattices of string-theory gauge groups.
