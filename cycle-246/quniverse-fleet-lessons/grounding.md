## Research idea

Operating the Quniverse Fleet: Objectives, Successes, Failures, and Roadmap of an Autonomous Cloud Research System. The Quniverse is a self-maintaining research and publishing system running on the Cloudflare Workers platform: thirty-six deployed workers (thirty-eight at the session's start) sharing ten SQL databases, eight vector indexes, nineteen object-storage buckets, and a metered model gateway, operated by an autonomous agent under human direction, with no code written by the human operator. This paper answers four questions in order: what the system actually is; what it was built to do; where it has succeeded and where it has failed across roughly a year of operation; and what the roadmap is.
