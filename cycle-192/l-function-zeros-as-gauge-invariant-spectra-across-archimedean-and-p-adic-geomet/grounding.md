## Research idea

L-function zeros as gauge-invariant spectra across Archimedean and p-adic geometries. The note conjectures that the tree (Bruhat–Tits/Stern–Brocot), the hyperbolic plane, and hierarchical ultrametric spaces are merely 'gauge choices' for one arithmetic object, with the L-function as the invariant core: zeros of L-functions should be the universal spectral invariants governing harmonic resonance on both the continuous (Maass forms on the modular surface) and discrete (tree-harmonic functions on p-adic buildings) sides. This is testable: numerically compare spectra of the Laplacian on the modular surface (Maass form eigenvalues) with spectra of the Laplacian on Bruhat–Tits trees for small primes, checking whether zero spacings of L(s,θ) match both; formally, attempt to prove that cross-ratio invariance under PGL(2,K) forces a common spectral measure across K=ℂ and K=ℚ_p. Confirming even partial correspondence would give a concrete discrete–continuous bridge beyond analogy; refuting it would bound the gauge-principle claim.
