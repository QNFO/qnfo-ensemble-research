## Research idea

Reflection Closure as Generator of All Physical Symmetries. Claim: starting from a single act of distinction (a line) and iteratively closing a set under reflections across its members, one generates precisely the Coxeter/dihedral symmetry groups — and the author conjectures this closure rule underlies every symmetry in nature. Test: formally enumerate closure sets of lines under mutual reflection in 2D and prove they coincide with finite Coxeter groups (I2(n), A2, etc.), then extend to hyperplane arrangements in n dimensions to recover the full finite Coxeter classification. Computationally, simulate random initial line configurations, apply the closure algorithm, and check which discrete groups emerge and with what frequencies. If closure always yields classified Coxeter systems, the conjecture gains mathematical substance: physical symmetries would be derivable from a minimal 'grammatical' rule of distinction plus reflection, with group structure depending only on initial conditions ('what you put in').
