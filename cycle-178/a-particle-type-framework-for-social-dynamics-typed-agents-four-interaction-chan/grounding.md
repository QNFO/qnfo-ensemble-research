## Research idea

A Particle-Physics Framework for Modeling Social Dynamics. The author conjectures that social systems can be formally modeled as a 'Standard Model of social particles': individuals typed as baryons (stable leaders), leptons (mobile information carriers), bosons (mediators transmitting interaction forces), and dark matter (unobserved populations), with four interaction forces (strong/family bonds, electromagnetic/like-dislike attraction, weak/random transformative events, gravity/unconscious cultural pull). The testable question: does this mapping yield predictive power beyond existing sociophysics models? A derivation or agent-based simulation could assign interaction potentials per particle type and force, then check whether simulated networks reproduce empirical social structures (clustering, influence cascades, mediation effects) better than baseline random or homogeneous-agent models, and whether 'weak-force' perturbations predict large behavioral shifts.
