## Research idea

Bruhat-Tits Tree Neural Architecture Where Decision Paths Are Explanations. Q-PNA conjecture: replacing continuous vector embeddings with a p-adic Bruhat-Tits tree (ultrametric geometry) makes the root-to-leaf decision path itself a faithful, verifiable explanation, eliminating post-hoc interpretability. The author reports a linear map plus tree-structured cophenetic loss reaching 20-40% hierarchical classification accuracy, beating a transformer 6.6x, with a token-calculus protocol catching 100% of corruptions. A paper could test this by: (1) proving that in ultrametric spaces any classification decision decomposes uniquely into a nested hierarchy of prefix decisions, so path explanations are exact rather than approximate; (2) formally characterizing when cophenetic-distance losses are differentiable and convex on the tree; (3) benchmarking against LIME/SHAP fidelity on hierarchical datasets to verify explanation faithfulness, and probing whether the accuracy advantage holds beyond narrow hierarchical tasks or is an artifact of weak baselines.
