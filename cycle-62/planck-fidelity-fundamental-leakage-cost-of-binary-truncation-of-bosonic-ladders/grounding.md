## Research idea

Planck Fidelity: Fundamental Leakage Cost of Binary Truncation of Bosonic Ladders. The transmon is a bosonic oscillator whose spectrum is a discrete but unbounded ladder; encoding it as a qubit (n∈{0,1}) is an artificial radix choice, not a physical necessity. Conjecture: for any finite-time gate driven by a raising operator a†, the 'Planck Loss' L_P = 1 − Tr(P_qubit |ψ⟩⟨ψ|) is strictly positive, making leakage a fundamental limit independent of materials or control, and imposing an entropy cost ~ln(d/2) for truncating d resolvable levels to two. This reframes the leakage crisis as evidence that quantum hardware should be built as base-d qudits rather than qubits. Testable: derive lower bounds on L_P for driven transmon Hamiltonians (e.g., via perturbation theory or leakage-error formalism), simulate coherent-drive population in n≥2 versus gate time and anharmonicity, and benchmark a 'Planck Fidelity' metric against measured leakage in transmons (e.g., d≈12 resolvable levels), comparing qudit-based versus qubit-based error correction overheads.
