# Adelic Factorization of Quantum Dynamics Across All Completions of ℚ: Consistency Conditions, Local Models, and a Quantitative Illustration

## Abstract

Quantum mechanics is formulated over the real numbers, yet Ostrowski's theorem shows that the rationals ℚ admit infinitely many completions: the Archimedean place ℝ and one p-adic field ℚₚ per prime. The specific gap addressed here is that no working consistency condition explains why the Archimedean factor dominates physical amplitudes or what the finite-prime factors would contribute. Following the proposal that Tate's 1950 thesis supplies a structural template for adelic quantum mechanics, we formulate a factorized amplitude $\mathcal{A}=\prod_v \mathcal{A}_v$ over all places, write the local path-integral factors, and state three consistency conditions: convergence of the infinite product, recovery of the real propagator when non-Archimedean factors are suppressed, and a functional-equation constraint analogous to the zeta functional equation. As exact arithmetic illustrations we verify the adelic product formula $|x|_\infty\prod_p|x|_p=1$ for $x=6,10,12$, and we aggregate the published gate fidelities of the Tianyan-287 superconducting processor (0.9990, 0.9956, 0.9870) into a mean $\bar{F}=0.993866\overline{6}$ and a product $F_{\text{prod}}=0.9816745428$, a gap of $0.0121921239$. Under the conjectured suppression law of prior adelic-Zitterbewegung work, the crossover length at which non-Archimedean amplitude suppression falls below $10^{-6}$ is $3.8632\times10^{-7}$ m. The work remains at the level of formal construction and explicit arithmetic; its practical value is a checklist of conditions any adelic quantum theory must satisfy.

## 1. Introduction

Every working quantum theory today assigns amplitudes to points of a real configuration space and evolves them by a real time parameter. The physicists who build and benchmark quantum processors, the theorists who derive spectra, and the engineers who certify devices all inherit this choice from classical mechanics, and none of the daily practice of quantum technology depends on questioning it. The cost of not questioning it is specific: if the choice of ℝ among the completions of ℚ is a convention rather than a necessity, then there should exist consistency conditions that single out the real sector, and those conditions would tell us what observable or structural signatures the discarded p-adic completions must leave behind. Without such conditions, proposals for physics beyond the standard formulation have no well-defined target: one cannot say what a p-adic correction to an interference experiment should look like, or how large it may be.

Ostrowski's theorem provides the mathematical starting point. Every non-trivial absolute value on ℚ is equivalent either to the standard Archimedean absolute value $|\cdot|_\infty$ or to a p-adic absolute value $|\cdot|_p$ for some prime $p$ [12]. The completions are therefore the real line and the fields $\mathbb{Q}_p$, one per prime, and the adele ring combines them into a single restricted product [12]. In number theory, Tate's 1950 thesis showed that a product of local zeta integrals over all places satisfies a global functional equation; the proposal that this structure serves as an explicit template for adelic quantum mechanics — local zeta integrals mapping to local quantum dynamics, the functional equation mapping to path-integral consistency — has been developed in a dedicated research programme [12], and a first formal statement of the factorization conjecture appears in prior work [9]. What is missing, and what this paper supplies, is a statement of the consistency conditions in operational form, an explicit verification of the one adelic identity that can be checked by elementary arithmetic, and a quantitative illustration of how local-place quantities combine under multiplicative aggregation.

The paper develops three tracks. First, we write the local path-integral factors $Z_\infty$ and $Z_p$ and the adelic product $Z_{\mathbb{A}}$, and we state the convergence, recovery, and functional-equation conditions that any completed theory must meet. Second, we verify by full arithmetic that the product formula $|x|_\infty\prod_p|x|_p=1$ holds exactly for $x=6,10,12$, the identity on which the conjectured suppression of non-Archimedean interference rests [10]. Third, we aggregate the published fidelities of a 105-qubit superconducting processor [2] into a mean and a product, computing every step, to illustrate how local-place metrics behave under adelic-style combination.

The contributions are:

- A statement of the adelic factorization conjecture for quantum amplitudes together with three explicit consistency conditions (convergence, real-sector recovery, functional-equation constraint), derived in Section 3.
- An exact arithmetic verification of the product formula $|x|_\infty\prod_p|x|_p=1$ for $x=6,10,12$, with every valuation computed, in Section 4.
- Computed aggregates of the processor fidelities of [2]: the mean $\bar{F}=0.993866\overline{6}$ and the product $F_{\text{prod}}=0.9816745428$, with their gap $0.0121921239$, derived in Section 4 and reported in Section 5.
- A derived crossover length $\ell=3.8632\times10^{-7}$ m at which the conjectured non-Archimedean suppression ratio $\lambda_C/\ell$ falls below $10^{-6}$, computed in Section 4 from the constants stated in [10], and discussed as a labeled projection in Section 7.

## 2. Background and Related Work

The adelic physics programme rests on a small set of mathematical results about how local objects at every place of ℚ combine into global ones. We review the works used here, stating for each the specific result its text establishes and the role it plays in our argument.

Tate's thesis as a template. The proposal that Tate's 1950 thesis provides an explicit structural template for constructing adelic quantum mechanics is stated and developed in [12]. Its text recalls the classification of local fields of characteristic zero as ℝ, ℂ, or finite extensions of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, defines the p-adic absolute value by writing $x=p^k\cdot a/b$ with $p\nmid a$, $p\nmid b$ and setting $|x|_p=p^{-k}$, and states the ultrametric inequality $|x+y|_p\leq\max(|x|_p,|y|_p)$, from which follow the qualitative differences between p-adic and real analysis: every triangle is isosceles, all balls are simultaneously open and closed, and there is no notion of connectedness [12]. It further defines the adele ring as the restricted direct product $\mathbb{A}_\mathbb{Q}=\mathbb{R}\times\prod_p'\mathbb{Q}_p$, in which all but finitely many p-adic components lie in the ring of integers, and lists four correspondences: local zeta integrals to local quantum dynamics, idele class group integration to global coherence via the product formula, the functional equation to path-integral consistency, and the archimedean factor to the distinguished role of measurement at the $\infty$-place [12]. The same text records that the Dragovich programme has realized the template for free (quadratic) theories via explicit adelic path integral constructions and that Huang, Stoica, and Zhong provided an independent proof-of-concept in conformal field theory [12]. We adopt these four correspondences as the organizing structure of our consistency conditions in Section 3.

The prior statement of the factorization conjecture. The conjecture that a global amplitude factorizes as $\mathcal{A}=\prod_{v\in\{\infty,2,3,5,\dots\}}\mathcal{A}_v$, with each factor living in the local field of place $v$, is stated in [9], together with the interpretation that the dominance of the Archimedean factor in ordinary experiments would be a consequence of a global consistency condition rather than a fundamental axiom [9]. That work also introduced the aggregation of device fidelities as a toy illustration of adelic products; we recompute those aggregates here with the same inputs and extend the arithmetic to the product formula itself. Our paper is a revision and tightening of [9], not a new theory.

Adelic measures controlling distribution across places. The arithmetic equidistribution theorem of Baker-Rumely and Favre-Rivera-Letelier, proved independently and generalized to curves by Chambert-Loir, establishes equidistribution of points of small height on the Berkovich compactification of the projective line with respect to an adelic measure on $\mathbb{P}^1$ [1]. Its proof machinery is explicit about the local contributions: for each place $v$ of the number field, the $\mu_v$-energy of the probability measure $[S_n]_v$ is given by a double sum involving the local Green's function $g_{\mu_v}(x,y)=-\log|\tilde{x}\wedge\tilde{y}|_v+G_{\mu_v}(\tilde{x})+G_{\mu_v}(\tilde{y})$, and the key lemma proves $\lim_{n\to\infty}([S_n]_v,[S_n]_v)_{\mu_v}=0$ at every place [1]. The text also develops the quasi-adelic condition, showing that scaling a local set $M_v$ rescales the capacity as $\operatorname{Cap}(rM_v)=r^2\operatorname{Cap}(M_v)$ and shifts the homogeneous potential by $\frac{1}{2}\log\operatorname{Cap}(M_v)$ [1]. This is the closest existing theorem to what our conjecture needs: a statement in which behavior at every place, Archimedean and non-Archimedean alike, is controlled by a single adelic object, with the local energies vanishing simultaneously. We use it in Section 3 as the structural analogy for what a convergence regularizer for $\prod_p Z_p$ would have to deliver.

Global rigidity from adelic line bundles. The arithmetic Hodge index theorem for adelic line bundles extends results from number fields to finitely generated fields over ℚ and applies the theorem to obtain a rigidity property of the sets of preperiodic points of polarizable algebraic dynamical systems [4]. The text sets up the group $\widehat{\mathrm{Pic}}(X)$ of isomorphism classes of adelic ℚ-line bundles over a projective variety over a finitely generated field $K$, defines a polarized dynamical system as a triple $(X,f,L)$ with $f^*L=qL$ for some rational $q>1$, and proves rigidity by showing that the admissible extensions $\overline{L}_f=\widehat{\ell}_f(\xi)$ and $\overline{M}_g=\widehat{\ell}_g(\xi)$ are nef, so that the fundamental inequality $\lambda_1^{\overline{H}}(X,\overline{N})\geq h_{\overline{N}}^{\overline{H}}(X)$ applies to their nef sum [4]. The relevance to our programme is the pattern: local adelic data, assembled with the right positivity, force a global statement (here, equality of line bundles on a Zariski-dense set of preperiodic points). Our conjecture seeks the analogous pattern for quantum amplitudes, and [4] is the demonstration that such global-from-local rigidity theorems are provable.

Adelic groups with a Lie theory. The category of finite-dimensional compact connected abelian groups (finite-dimensional protori) is equivalent to the category of adelic tori, and for each adelic torus $G$ there is a proper short exact sequence $0\to\mathbb{Q}^n\to\mathcal{L}(G)\xrightarrow{\exp}G\to 0$ with $\exp$ the adelic exponential [6]. The construction proceeds through ultraproducts: a nonprincipal ultrafilter on the primes yields a field $\widetilde{\mathbb{K}}=\prod_{\mathfrak{u}}\mathbb{F}_p$ of characteristic zero and cardinality continuum containing an algebraic closure of its prime field, built via the Frobenius density theorem and the ultrafilter theorem [6], and the ultraproduct $\widetilde{\mathbb{Z}}=\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}/\mathfrak{u}$ of $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}=\prod_{p}\mathbb{Z}_p$ is a valuation domain [6]. This supplies the concrete Lie-theoretic language in which an adelic phase space for a quantum system would have to live; the exact sequence with the adelic exponential is the object one would differentiate to obtain adelic commutators.

Counting over discrete structures. Under the Impartial Anonymous Culture assumption, the probability of a voting event is computed by counting integral solutions to a system of linear inequalities, and Ehrhart theory provides the machinery; symmetry reductions of the linear systems allow exact limiting probabilities for large elections with four candidates, and the text anticipates software for Ehrhart quasi-polynomials that would yield explicit, computer-assisted formulas for voting outcomes [5]. The methodological parallel is direct: summing contributions over a discrete lattice with local weights is exactly the structure a truncated adelic product over primes has, and [5] shows that such sums become tractable when symmetry is exploited. We use this in Section 4 when we truncate the infinite product to finitely many places and note that the truncation is the point where Ehrhart-style counting would enter a serious computation.

Quantum simulation of dynamical systems. The quantum sawtooth map, a kicked system governed by the Hamiltonian $H(\theta,I;\tau)=I^2/2+V(\theta)\sum_j\delta(\tau-jT)$, can be simulated on a quantum computer with an algorithm built on the forward/backward quantum Fourier transform between momentum and angle bases, and the localization length of the system can be extracted with quadratic speedup over classical simulation [7]. The text concludes that a quantum computer with a small number of qubits can efficiently simulate quantum localization effects and compute the fidelity of quantum motion, and that such algorithms are accessible to first-generation processors with fewer than 10 qubits [7]. This matters for our test programme: any observable signature predicted by an adelic theory would most plausibly be hunted with exactly these dynamical-systems algorithms, since fidelity and localization are the quantities they already extract.

Consistent hybrid dynamics. Consistent dynamics coupling classical and quantum degrees of freedom exists provided it is stochastic: the resulting dynamics is linear in the hybrid state, completely positive, and trace preserving, and it avoids the pathologies of semiclassical equations, with an application to the back-reaction of quantum fields on spacetime [8]. Its toy models exhibit three features: stochastic collapses of the quantum degrees of freedom, a trade-off between classical diffusion in phase space and quantum decoherence, and non-conservation of energy, which the text discusses as a possible experimental signature of fundamental hybrid dynamics, controllable at leading order [8]. If an adelic quantum theory couples an Archimedean sector to non-Archimedean ones, the hybrid formalism of [8] is the existing template for a consistent composite dynamics, and energy non-conservation is the kind of signature one should expect at the interface.

Quantum hardware as the measurement platform. The Tianyan-287 cloud-accessible superconducting processor features 105 qubits with single-qubit gate, two-qubit gate, and readout fidelities of 99.90%, 99.56%, and 98.7% respectively [2]. Its text describes a random circuit sampling experiment with 74 qubits and 24 cycles using iSWAP-like two-qubit gates in the pattern ABCD–CDAB per cycle, achieving quantum advantage beyond existing top supercomputers and marking the first commercial cloud access to leading quantum computing power, while noting that RCS tasks based on iSWAP-like gates have limited practical application [2]. We use the three fidelity numbers as the only empirical inputs in this paper, and the platform itself as the concrete class of hardware on which any near-term adelic signature search would run.

Adjacent frameworks and their status. A ratio-based adelic framework has been proposed that replaces integer-prime models with generalized scaling ratios $q$ and Bruhat-Tits tree topologies, and that proposes laboratory corroboration through measuring precision $q$-adic scaling arrays in photonic spin glasses and searching cosmic microwave background data for log-periodic signatures generated by tree-branching scale limits [11]. We cite [11] for these proposed experiments, which are the nearest published experimental proposals in the adelic-physics neighbourhood, while noting that its framework is stated at the level of mathematical ontology rather than of derivable amplitudes. Separately, an adelic extension of Zitterbewegung has been proposed in which the trembling motion of a free Dirac particle decomposes into an Archimedean contribution at $\omega_\infty=2m_ec^2/\hbar=1.5521\times10^{21}\,\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}$ and one contribution per p-adic sector, suppressed at macroscopic scales by the product formula, which that work verifies exactly for $x=6,10,12$; it states the conjectured suppression law $A_{\mathrm{int}}/A_0=\lambda_C/\ell$ explicitly as a conjecture, with the assessment that "the arithmetic is exact; the physical content is the conjecture itself" and that the adelic Dirac equation is postulated, not derived [10]. We adopt the same epistemic discipline: our Section 4 verifies the exact arithmetic, and the physical identification remains conjectural.

## 3. Methods

The method is a formal construction in three tracks, each ending in a checkable condition. We state every definition and every condition; nothing in this section is claimed as a proven physical result.
