## Research idea

THz-Driven Synthetic-Dimension Lattice in TFLN with Thermal-Photon Noise Limits. Conjecture: a 3.5 THz drive in thin-film lithium niobate can dynamically synthesize a 4D lattice (via the Pockels effect) through which 1550 nm 'traveler' photons propagate, and the operating temperature is set by a thermodynamic criterion: the drive must exceed the thermal photon occupation at 3.5 THz, requiring cryogenic operation (~80 K). A paper could test this by (1) deriving the effective Hamiltonian mapping the THz-modulated electro-optic response onto a synthetic-dimension tight-binding model, (2) computing the thermal photon occupancy n(ν,T) at 3.5 THz for 300 K vs 80 K to quantify noise-induced decoherence of the synthetic lattice, and (3) simulating traveler-photon transport fidelity as a function of drive power, temperature, and lattice depth to establish feasibility thresholds for a purely photonic interaction engine.
