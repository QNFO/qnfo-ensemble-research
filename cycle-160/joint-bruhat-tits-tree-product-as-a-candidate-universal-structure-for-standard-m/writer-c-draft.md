# The Joint Bruhat–Tits Tree $T_{2,3,5}$ as a Candidate Universal Structure: Density Analysis, Falsifiable Predictions, and the Gauge-Group Gap

## Abstract

We examine the conjecture that the product of the Bruhat–Tits trees $T_2$, $T_3$, $T_5$, diagonally embedded into the positive reals through the Pythagorean semigroup $P=\{2^a 3^b 5^c : a,b,c\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}\}$, is a single geometric object underlying Standard Model mass ratios, GKP quantum error correction lattices, holographic bulk geometry, and Efimov-type log-periodic scaling. We first establish an uncomfortable baseline: because $\ln 2/\ln 3$ is irrational, $P$ is dense in $\mathbb{R}_+$, so any finite set of mass ratios can be approximated arbitrarily well by semigroup elements. We quantify this obstruction with explicit counting: the number of semigroup elements below $10^4$ is $172$, the local log-density near the muon-to-electron mass ratio $m_\mu/m_e = 206.7683$ is $11.5973$ per unit of $\ln X$, and the expected number of semigroup elements within $\pm 5\%$ of any fixed ratio near that scale is $1.1317$. Consequently a naive "all eleven ratios fit" claim carries only binomial significance $\approx 4.88\times 10^{-4}$ under a conservative null, and less at higher ratios. We then reformulate the conjecture's testable content: (i) a radiative-correction model in which the $\sim 1\%$ residuals are explained by $p$-adic valuation structure rather than by free fitting; (ii) a predicted photon-loss hierarchy in GKP codes governed by $p$-adic valuations $\mathrm{ord}_p(k)$; (iii) stepwise entanglement entropy in $|\log_2 L|$; and (iv) gauge-group emergence from tree automorphisms, which remains the conjecture's main unproven pillar. We argue that (i)–(iii) are falsifiable, while the density result permanently weakens purely numerological mass fits.

## 1. Introduction

A recurring temptation in mathematical physics is to find a small set of integers whose products reproduce the constants of nature. The program studied here [9], [10] makes an unusually structural version of this claim: not that masses are *numbers* of a special form, but that they are *positions on a geometry* — the product of the Bruhat–Tits trees $T_2$, $T_3$, $T_5$, with the diagonal embedding into $\mathbb{R}_+$ realized by the Pythagorean semigroup

$$P=\{2^a\,3^b\,5^c : a,b,c\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}\}.$$

A Bruhat–Tits tree $T_p$ is the infinite $(p+1)$-regular tree associated to the field $\mathbb{Q}_p$; its boundary is $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{Q}_p)$, and its automorphism group contains $PGL_2(\mathbb{Q}_p)$. The conjecture of [9], [10] asserts that this triple product simultaneously organizes the Standard Model mass spectrum, the shift-lattice structure of GKP quantum error-correcting codes, the effective bulk geometry of certain holographic duals, and the log-periodic tower of Efimov physics.

Such a claim must survive two very different audits. The first is statistical: do the mass ratios really prefer $P$ over chance? The second is architectural: does the tree geometry *predict* anything a numerological fit does not? In this paper we carry out both audits as far as is currently possible. Our central negative-but-constructive result (Section 4) is a density analysis showing that $P$ is dense in $\mathbb{R}_+$, that the local density of semigroup elements on the logarithmic line grows as $(\ln X)^2$, and therefore that agreement between measured ratios and semigroup elements is *a priori likely* rather than surprising. This sharpens, with explicit arithmetic, the "semigroup density epistemological risk" already acknowledged in the v3.2 corrections of [10]. We then isolate the parts of the conjecture that remain falsifiable despite density: the valuation structure of the residuals, the $p$-adic photon-loss hierarchy in GKP codes, and the stepwise entanglement entropy scaling. Throughout, we distinguish sharply between what is computed here, what is projected under stated assumptions, and what is conjecture.

## 2. Background and Related Work

The provided literature spans combinatorics, computation, and physics; we discuss each item and its relation to the tree-geometry conjecture.

**[1] (arXiv:0804.4584v1, Feature Unification in TAG Derivation Trees).** This work defines feature-based regular tree grammars and a translation from feature-based tree adjoining grammars that *preserves derivation structures*. The relevance is methodological: derivation trees carrying linguistic features are a concrete instance of "tree plus labeling data" — exactly the shape of the claim that mass ratios label vertices of $T_{2,3,5}$. It shows that tree-structure-preserving encodings of extra data can be made formal and lossless, a standard the mass-ratio conjecture does not yet meet.

**[2] (arXiv:2209.09166v2, Cache-Oblivious Representation of B-Tree Structures).** CORoBTS stores B-tree-like search trees using a van Emde Boas layout combined with a packed memory array, and analyzes depth-first search complexity. For us this matters in two ways: it demonstrates that the *same* tree admits radically different, provably efficient embeddings into linear memory, and it supplies the computational analogy for the diagonal embedding $\prod_p T_p \to \mathbb{R}_+$ — an embedding whose efficiency (here, statistical distinguishability) must be analyzed, not assumed.

**[3] (arXiv:1504.02243v2, Spanning structures and universality in sparse hypergraphs).** This paper adapts Riordan's spanning-subgraph results from random graphs to random $r$-uniform hypergraphs, giving sufficient conditions for universality of spanning structures. Universality in sparse random structures is the correct probabilistic lens for our Section 4: a dense semigroup hit against eleven measured ratios is a hitting problem, and hypergraph universality results caution that "everything appears somewhere" is the generic outcome in sparse-but-dense combinatorial settings.

**[4] (arXiv:2007.01051v2, Universal Scaling of Electron Transmission).** Using non-equilibrium Green's function methods, this work predicts two universal scaling regimes for transmission $\mathcal{T}(E)$ in disordered metallic nanodevices. It is a model of what the tree conjecture lacks: a *derived* universal scaling law with a specified regime boundary. Our proposed GKP photon-loss hierarchy (Section 3) is modeled on this pattern — a scaling exponent tied to a structural integer ($\mathrm{ord}_p(k)$) rather than to a fit.

**[5] (arXiv:2009.07197v4, Universal Mass Scale for Bosonic Fields in Multi-Brane Worlds).** This paper derives a universal mass scale for all $q$-form fields in multi-brane models, requiring covariant multi-localization to obtain an ultralight mode. It is the closest physics analogue to the mass-spectrum pillar of the conjecture: a geometric setup (extra branes) producing a *universal* mass organization. Notably, its mass scale is derived from geometry and covariance, not selected from a semigroup — illustrating the derivation standard the gauge-group pillar of [9] must eventually reach.

**[6] (arXiv:2512.19623v2, Learning Enables Exponential-to-Polynomial Sampling Overhead Scaling in Quantum Divide-and-Conquer for Tree-Structured Circuits).** For tree-structured circuits, learning reduces the sampling overhead of circuit cutting from exponential to polynomial in the number of cut locations. This is directly relevant to the GKP/testability pillar: any claim that tree geometry organizes a quantum code must eventually pay a sampling cost to verify, and [6] shows that structure-adaptive learning can change that cost's asymptotics — a template for how the $|\log_2 L|$ entropy-step prediction could be efficiently certified on hardware.

**[7] (arXiv:2311.03640v1, Ce 4f states in CeIr$_3$).** Photoelectron spectroscopy of the intermediate-valence superconductor CeIr$_3$ shows Ce $4f$ states near the Fermi energy, suggesting itinerant character. We cite it as a cautionary physical example: an apparently "universal" spectral feature (valence fluctuations) explained by conventional band physics. Before invoking tree geometry for mass ratios, ordinary dynamics — radiative corrections, renormalization group flow — must be excluded as the explanation of residuals, which is precisely the program of Section 3.1.

**[8] (arXiv:1808.09078v2, A Universal Bijection for Catalan Structures).** A Catalan magma is a unique-factorisation normed magma with one irreducible element; the paper characterizes when a set with a product map is a free magma on its irreducibles, yielding a universal bijection across Catalan-enumerated structures. This is the deepest structural echo of our subject: $P$ is a free commutative monoid on three irreducibles $\{2,3,5\}$, and [8] supplies the general theorem conditions under which unique factorisation into irreducibles generates a rigid, universally comparable structure. The conjecture of [9] is, in this language, the claim that physics "factors uniquely" over three primes.

**[9] (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21498074, Phase 3–4 Update).** The cross-domain synthesis asserting that $P$ is simultaneously the SM mass spectrum, the GKP code lattice, the Efimov discretuum, and the diagonal embedding of $T_{2,3,5}$, with a 15-entry calibration register. This is the primary target of our audit.

**[10] (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21754154, v3.2 corrections).** The corrected version, notable for intellectual honesty: arithmetic errors in mass-ratio triplets were corrected with 5 of 9 verified independently, the Efimov $\lambda$ derivation was replaced by an "honest structural correspondence," and the semigroup density epistemological risk was acknowledged. Our Section 4 turns that acknowledgment into quantitative arithmetic.

**[11] (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21991953, Quantum Laws of Form).** Synthesizes Spencer-Brown's distinction-based construction of logic with quantum correspondences, identifying open problem categories. It supplies the philosophical frame in which a single combinatorial primitive (here, tree vertices; there, distinctions) is asked to generate physics — and the caution that such programs succeed or fail at the level of *derived dynamics*, not static classification.

**[12] (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21756190, Ostrowski Dimensionless Reformulation).** Compiles 53 fundamental equations in dimensionless Planck units, motivated by the observation that dimensional formulation privileges the Archimedean completion of $\mathbb{Q}$ per Ostrowski's theorem (1916). This is the precise mathematical home of our embedding: Ostrowski's theorem classifies completions of $\mathbb{Q}$ into the Archimedean $\mathbb{R}$ and the $p$-adic $\mathbb{Q}_p$; the tree conjecture proposes that physics has been reading only the Archimedean shadow of a structure living on all completions at once.

## 3. Methods

### 3.1 The object and the embedding

For each prime $p\in\{2,3,5\}$, let $T_p$ denote the Bruhat–Tits tree of $\mathbb{Q}_p$, the $(p+1)$-regular tree whose boundary is $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{Q}_p)$. The joint tree is the product $T_{2,3,5}=T_2\times T_3\times T_5$. The diagonal embedding into the positive reals is realized at the level of the multiplicative group: an element of the semigroup $P=\{2^a3^b5^c\}$ maps to the real number $2^a3^b5^c\in\mathbb{R}_+$, i.e., to the point $\ln X = a\ln 2 + b\ln 3 + c\ln 5$ on the logarithmic line. Per Ostrowski's theorem [12], this selects three non-Archimedean completions and one Archimedean target; the conjecture [9] is that physical dimensionless quantities are supported on the image.

### 3.2 Statistical protocol for the mass-ratio pillar

Let $\rho_i$, $i=1,\dots,11$, be the measured dimensionless mass ratios in the calibration register of [9] (e.g., $\rho_1 = m_\mu/m_e$). The null hypothesis $H_0$ is that each $\rho_i$ is an arbitrary point on the logarithmic line; the alternative $H_1$ is that each $\rho_i$ lies within tolerance $\delta$ of an element of $P$, with residuals explained by a *structured* radiative-correction model (Section 3.3). The test statistic is the count of hits $K=\sum_i \mathbf{1}[\exists\, x\in P:\ |\ln x - \ln\rho_i| \le \delta]$. Under $H_0$, the per-ratio hit probability $f_i$ is *not* a free parameter: it is fixed by the semigroup density computed in Section 4. This is the key methodological discipline — the density arithmetic determines the null distribution, removing the researcher's freedom to choose a favorable chance level.

### 3.3 Radiative-correction model (stated as a hypothesis)

Hypothesis H-R: the residual $\Delta_i = \ln\rho_i - \ln x_i$ (with $x_i\in P$ the nearest semigroup element) is not free but takes the form

$$\Delta_i = \sum_{p\in\{2,3,5\}} c_p\, p^{-\,\mathrm{ord}_p(q_i)},$$

where $q_i$ is a small integer characterizing the loop order or mixing channel of ratio $i$, $\mathrm{ord}_p(q_i)$ is the $p$-adic valuation of $q_i$, and $c_p$ are constants fixed once across all ratios.
