## Research idea

Passive Quantum Error Correction via Ultrametric Geometry. Claim: encoding quantum information in p-adic (ultrametric) spaces with Bruhat-Tits tree structure yields passive fault tolerance—the strong triangle inequality geometrically confines errors within discrete boundaries, so energy barriers scale exponentially with tree depth (E ∝ q^d), eliminating the need for active surface-code correction and its thermodynamic overhead. This matters because active error correction is a leading scalability bottleneck for quantum hardware. Testable path: (1) formally define a p-adic code space and prove an error-confinement theorem showing local perturbations cannot propagate across ultrametric distance classes; (2) derive the energy-barrier scaling from the tree geometry and compare Landauer/cooling budgets against surface-code overhead; (3) simulate noise channels (depolarizing, drift) on quantum walks over Bruhat-Tits trees versus Euclidean lattices, measuring logical error rates and correction energy per qubit as depth grows.
