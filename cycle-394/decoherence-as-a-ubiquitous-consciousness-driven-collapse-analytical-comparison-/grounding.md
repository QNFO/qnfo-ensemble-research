## Research idea

Decoherence as Ubiquitous Consciousness-Driven Collapse. Conjecture: if consciousness is fundamental and causes wavefunction collapse (Wigner), then collapse occurs everywhere continuously, and this 'everywhere collapse' should be empirically indistinguishable from environmental decoherence. The environment acts as a collective low-grade conscious field that 'observes' the system. This unifies the measurement problem and the hard problem of consciousness as one phenomenon. Testability: derive whether a consciousness-mediated collapse model predicts decoherence rates identical to standard Lindblad/master-equation treatments for mesoscopic systems (e.g., interferometry with large molecules), or whether it yields detectable deviations—e.g., collapse-rate scaling with system complexity rather than with environmental coupling. A formal analysis could compare Diósi-Penrose-style objective collapse mathematics with a panpsychist collapse ansatz, and simulations could check whether such deviations are within current experimental bounds (e.g., matter-wave experiments, superconducting qubits).
