## Research idea

Ultrametricity as a Consequence of Distinction-Calculus Nesting. Claim: the strong triangle condition (ultrametricity) is not an imposed metric axiom but a consistency requirement of any calculus of nested distinctions (Spencer-Brown's Laws of Form): all valid forms reduce to trees, so hierarchical/ultrametric structure should emerge generically in systems built from recursive distinction-making (spin-glass state overlap, angular-ordered parton showers, RG scale trees, molecular-clock phylogenies, subitizing-based chunking in cognition). Open question: is the convergence across these domains structural identity or analogy? Testable paths: (1) formal derivation showing the Calling/Crossing axioms imply the ultrametric inequality, and that multi-valued distinctions yield k-ary trees matching Bruhat-Tits branching p; (2) simulations of recursive chunking with a subitizing limit (~4 items) checking whether overlap matrices of resulting categories satisfy ultrametricity quantitatively; (3) numerical tests (e.g., p-adic or RSB overlap data, jet substructure trees) for deviations from ultrametricity in systems with overlapping, non-nested interactions, bounding the claim's domain of validity.
