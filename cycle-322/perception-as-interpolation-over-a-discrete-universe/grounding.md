## Research idea

Perception as Interpolation Over a Discrete Universe. The author conjectures that human perception and cognition function fundamentally as interpolation: the brain receives discrete, incomplete data packets from a fundamentally discrete (quantized) universe and fills the gaps to render a continuous, seamless experience. This extends to free will: the felt sense of choosing arises because the predictive architecture simulates multiple interpolated outcomes. A paper could formalize this by modeling perception as a reconstruction operator mapping discrete samples to continuous percepts, testing whether known illusions (blind-spot filling, phi phenomenon, phonemic restoration) match predictions of specific interpolation kernels, and whether the smoothness of conscious experience is bounded by sampling-theoretic limits of neural input.
