## Research idea

Emergent Cognitive Time from Ultrametric Constraints via Page–Wootters Conditioning. Conjecture: the experience of temporal flow in cognition can be derived without any time primitive, by treating the brain's state space as a static, constraint-satisfied web (ultrametric overlap matrix Q obeying a cocycle condition, analogous to H|Ψ⟩=0) and extracting ordered 'evolution' purely by conditioning the semantic network S on the internally ordered states φ of a reference neural population R (e.g., theta phase). Claim: the sequence of conditional overlap matrices Q(φ₁), Q(φ₂), … reproduces stable percepts and reaction-time statistics, with 'later' defined only by R's own correlation structure. This matters because it dissolves the circularity of explaining time with clocks and unifies quantum gravity's problem of time with cognitive dynamics. Test: formalize the joint constrained distribution P(Q,φ) compatible with ultrametricity; prove that conditioning yields a consistent ordered narrative (Page–Wootters consistency conditions); simulate a neural-oscillator-plus-semantic-network model and check whether reaction times scale with reference-state steps and whether conditional Q(φ) trajectories jump across the ultrametric tree as observed percepts do.
