## Research idea

Completion-Invariance of Physical Ontology: Rational Base Field in Quantum Foundations. The physical content of quantum mechanics resides in rational measurement outcomes; ℝ and ℚ_p are equally instrumental completions of a rational base field, so ontology must be invariant under the choice of completion. This 'completion-invariance' principle forbids realism about the continuum while legitimizing adelic methods as calculational rather than ontological. It matters because it converts a metaphysical dispute into a sharp formal question: do standard quantum predictions depend on the archimedean completion at all? A paper could (1) formulate a finite-precision/rational quantum mechanics and prove it reproduces standard predictions for all experimentally accessible observables, with deviations bounded by measurement precision; (2) identify regimes where ℝ and ℚ_p completions genuinely diverge (e.g., scale-invariant or ultrametric regimes) and determine whether any divergence is observable in principle; (3) formalize the author's symmetry caveat as a theorem: any realist argument for ℝ-reality transfers mutatis mutandis to ℚ_p-reality, so consistent realism must accept both completions or neither.
