## Research idea

Anisotropic Fine-Structure Constant from Non-Isotropic p-adic Embeddings. Conjecture: if the embedding of p-adic completions into the archimedean sector is not isotropic—different spatial directions coupling to different primes—then the fine-structure constant α acquires a directional dependence, detectable as orientation-dependent shifts in quantum metrological standards (Josephson constant KJ=2e/h, von Klitzing constant RK=h/e²). This matters because it converts an abstract number-theoretic geometric assumption into a falsifiable laboratory prediction, with existing Lorentz-violation/SME bounds at the 10⁻²⁰ level already constraining the anisotropy. A formal analysis could derive how prime-direction coupling modifies the electromagnetic coupling tensor and whether e and h shift in ways that cancel in KJ and RK; simulations could model periodic modulations of quantum-Hall-to-conventional resistance ratios as the apparatus rotates relative to a preferred frame (e.g., the CMB rest frame), yielding quantitative bounds on p-adic embedding isotropy.
