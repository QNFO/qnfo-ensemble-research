## Research idea

Zitterbewegung as Archimedean–2-adic Channel Mixing. The note conjectures that Zitterbewegung — the Compton-frequency oscillation of the Dirac velocity operator — is not merely an interference of positive/negative energy branches on ℝ, but the physical signature of mixing between the Archimedean completion of ℚ and the 2-adic completion: a localized wave packet at x∞ necessarily contains Fourier components delocalized at x₂, with the Majorana condition realizing identification of the ∞ and 2 places and topological protection arising from the Ostrowski-incommensurability of ℝ and ℚ₂. This matters because it would give an observable (Compton-scale) empirical handle on whether p-adic sectors of the adeles enter physics, and would explain why ZBW survives representation changes (Foldy-Wouthuysen) as an ontological rather than coordinate effect. A derivation could test it by formulating the Dirac equation on the adele ring, computing the ∞↔2 transition amplitude for localized packets, and checking that the predicted oscillation frequency 2E/ħ and its persistence under FW transformation follow from the adelic product formula; simulation of p-adic Schrödinger dynamics coupled to a real sector could look for the predicted channel-mixing signatures, comparable to Gerritsen-style trapped-ion emulation.
