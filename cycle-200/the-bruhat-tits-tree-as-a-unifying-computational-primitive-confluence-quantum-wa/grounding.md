## Research idea

Bruhat-Tits Tree as a Unifying Computational Primitive. The author conjectures that the p-adic Bruhat-Tits tree is a single computational primitive underlying three domains: ultrametric quantum computing (p-adic amplitudes on the tree), LLM tokenization (tokens as prime products whose divisibility induces an ultrametric semantic hierarchy), and theorem proving (a distinction calculus whose boundary reductions normalize proofs on the same tree, with self-reference handled as oscillation rather than contradiction). Testable claims: (1) a transformer using tree-distance attention over p-adic token embeddings is more sample-efficient than dot-product attention; (2) p-adic quantum walks on the tree converge faster than classical analogues; (3) distinction-calculus reduction rules are provably confluent and terminating, yielding unique normal forms usable as a choice-free proof kernel for verifying braid/circuit equivalence. A paper could formalize the shared tree structure, prove confluence, and run tokenizer and quantum-walk simulations.
