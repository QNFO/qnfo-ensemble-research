## Research idea

Quantifying Unification Gain via Algorithmic Information Theory. The author conjectures that structural unification (S-type) contributions can be quantified by an Algorithmic Information Theory metric, the 'Unification Gain' G_U: the reduction in total description (Kolmogorov) complexity achieved when disparate theories are subsumed under one framework. This would give non-predictive theoretical work an objective validation metric, addressing the problem that unification lacks falsifiability. A formal analysis could define G_U = K(T1) + K(T2) − K(U) for unified theory U, prove properties (non-negativity, invariance under encoding), and test it computationally on historical cases (Maxwell, thermodynamics/statistical mechanics, renormalization group) by compressing formalized theory representations, comparing measured G_U against consensus judgments of unificatory value.
