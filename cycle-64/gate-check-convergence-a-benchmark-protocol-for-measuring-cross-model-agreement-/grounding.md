## Research idea

Measuring LLM Gate-Check Convergence on Fringe Physics Claims. Claim: independent LLMs evaluating novel scientific claims exhibit convergent 'gate-check' behavior — reaching identical verdicts via shared reasoning scaffolds (e.g., Bell's theorem, SU(2)/SO(3), BCS gap arithmetic) — suggesting models retrieve a common consensus lattice rather than pattern-matching style. Question: is this convergence robust across claim domains, model families, and prompt framings, and does it correlate with accuracy against expert ground truth? Test: construct a benchmark of fringe claims with known expert verdicts (falsified/trivial/valid-but-flawed), run blinded evaluations across multiple LLMs, and quantify inter-model agreement (e.g., Cohen's kappa on verdicts and argument-structure alignment) plus citation-quality variance. Formal analysis could model convergence as retrieval from overlapping training corpora, predicting higher agreement on well-represented consensus results than on frontier questions — a falsifiable signature distinguishing genuine gate-checking from correlated hallucination.
