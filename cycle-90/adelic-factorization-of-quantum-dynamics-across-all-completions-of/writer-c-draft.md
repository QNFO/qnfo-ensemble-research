# Adelic Factorization of Quantum Dynamics Across All Completions of $\mathbb{Q}$: A Consistency Programme

## Abstract

Ostrowski's theorem partitions the non-trivial completions of $\mathbb{Q}$ into exactly one Archimedean place ($\mathbb{R}$) and infinitely many non-Archimedean places ($\mathbb{Q}_p$ for each prime $p$), yet physical quantum mechanics is formulated exclusively over $\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathbb{C}$. We treat the choice of $\mathbb{R}$ as a convention to be tested rather than a necessity, and formulate a consistency programme in which quantum dynamics admits local factors at every place of $\mathbb{Q}$, constrained to cohere by a global adelic condition analogous to the functional equation that Tate's thesis derives from adelic Poisson summation. We make the programme precise through three concrete instruments: (i) the adelic product formula on $\mathbb{Q}^\times$, verified here by explicit computation; (ii) the completed Riemann zeta identity $\Lambda(s) = \zeta_\infty(s)\prod_p \zeta_p(s)$, whose functional equation we verify numerically at $s = 2$ and $s = -1$, giving $\Lambda(2) = \Lambda(-1) = \pi/6$; and (iii) an explicit tail bound showing that finite-prime contributions to such spectral identities are controllable, with the tail over primes $p > 100$ bounded above by $e^{0.010002} \approx 1.01006$. We survey the adelic-mathematics and quantum-dynamics literature that supplies the structural template, derive a quantitative error-budget illustration for cloud quantum hardware as the experimental baseline, and state the failure modes under which the programme would be falsified.

## 1. Introduction

The real numbers enter quantum mechanics as if by fiat: Hilbert spaces are over $\mathbb{C} \cong \mathbb{R} \oplus i\mathbb{R}$, observables are self-adjoint real-valued operators, and probabilities are real. But the field $\mathbb{Q}$ of rationals — the field every experimental readout ultimately lives in — admits other completions. Ostrowski's theorem partitions all non-trivial absolute values on $\mathbb{Q}$ into exactly one Archimedean completion, $\mathbb{R}$, sitting at the place $\infty$, and infinitely many non-Archimedean completions $\mathbb{Q}_p$, one for each prime $p$ [11]. These topologies are mutually singular: no sequence converges simultaneously in two distinct completions unless it is eventually constant [11]. Nothing in the axioms of quantum theory singles out the Archimedean place by logical necessity; the choice is a convention inherited from the historical development of analysis on $\mathbb{R}$.

This paper does not claim to construct a full adelic quantum theory. It does something more modest and more verifiable: it states the conjecture that quantum dynamics should factorize into local contributions at every place of $\mathbb{Q}$, it identifies the global consistency conditions that such a factorization must satisfy by analogy with the local-global structure of Tate's thesis, and it reduces the conjecture to three concrete instruments whose status can be checked with explicit arithmetic:

1. **The product formula.** For every $x \in \mathbb{Q}^\times$, the adelic norm satisfies $\prod_v |x|_v = 1$, where $v$ runs over $\infty$ and all primes. This is the prototype of a "global constraint from local data" and we verify it by direct computation in Section 4.

2. **The completed zeta identity.** The completed Riemann zeta function $\Lambda(s) = \zeta_\infty(s) \prod_p \zeta_p(s)$ satisfies the functional equation $\Lambda(s) = \Lambda(1-s)$. In Tate's thesis this identity emerges from adelic Poisson summation; it is the structural template for the conjecture that a physically admissible spectrum must satisfy a functional equation tying its Archimedean and non-Archimedean factors together [9]. We verify the identity at a pair of symmetric points by explicit arithmetic.

3. **Tail control.** Any numerical test of an adelic spectral identity must truncate the product over primes. We derive an explicit, rigorous upper bound on the truncated tail, so that future numerical studies know exactly how much of the product they may ignore.

The significance of the programme, if it succeeds, is twofold. Structurally, it would explain why $\mathbb{R}$ appears in physics: the Archimedean factor would be one local constituent of a globally coherent object, not the whole story. Empirically, it would predict observable or structural consequences of the finite-prime factors — for example, in the spectra of quantum systems, in quantum error-correcting codes, or in gauge structure, following the falsifiable predications proposed in the Depth–Breadth–Valuation ontology [12].

The paper is organized as follows. Section 2 surveys the related literature. Section 3 sets out the formal framework and definitions. Section 4 carries out all explicit derivations with every arithmetic step shown. Section 5 reports the results. Section 6 discusses limitations, failure modes, and falsification criteria. Section 7 concludes.

## 2. Background and Related Work

We discuss the works in the supplied bibliography in order. Because the bibliography mixes number-theoretic, quantum-informational, and conceptual sources, we group them by role in the argument.

**Adelic structures in arithmetic dynamics.** Baker, Rumely, Favre, Rivera-Letelier and Chambert-Loir's equidistribution theorem [1] established that points of small height on the Berkovich compactification of $\mathbb{P}^1$ equidistribute with respect to an adelic measure; the summary supplied records that Baker–Rumely and Favre–Rivera–Letelier proved this independently, and that Chambert-Loir proved a more general version for curves from a different approach. The relevance to our programme is methodological: an *adelic measure* — a choice of measure at every place simultaneously — is already the fundamental data in a mature field of arithmetic dynamics, and equidistribution theorems show that the global adelic object controls asymptotic behavior visible at each place. Our conjecture transfers exactly this pattern to quantum dynamics: the physical state space would carry an adelic measure-like structure whose global coherence constrains local (real-valued) observables.

The arithmetic Hodge index theorem for adelic line bundles [4] extends results from number fields to finitely generated fields, using the theory of adelic line bundles, and applies the theorem to obtain a rigidity property of the sets of preperiodic points of polarizable algebraic dynamical systems. This is a second precedent of the same shape: an adelic global object (a line bundle with local data at every place) satisfies an index theorem whose consequence is *rigidity* — the global condition severely constrains the local dynamical systems. If quantum dynamics factorizes adelically, one should expect analogous rigidity: the adelic consistency conditions should constrain which local (real) quantum theories are admissible.

**Adelic groups and Lie theory.** The identity of finite-dimensional protori with adelic tori [6] shows that the category of finite-dimensional compact connected abelian groups *is* the category of adelic tori, equipped with a complete Lie theory: for each adelic torus $G$ there is a proper short exact sequence $0 \to \mathbb{Q}^n \to \mathcal{L}(G) \xrightarrow{\exp} G \to 0$ with $\exp$ the adelic exponential, and the work extends the definition of nonarchimedean dimension. This matters directly for our programme: it demonstrates that the standard apparatus of Lie theory — Lie algebras, exponentials, exact sequences — exists over the adeles, not merely over $\mathbb{R}$. Any adelic quantum theory will need unitary groups and their Lie algebras at every place; [6] supplies evidence that this apparatus is not formal but fully structural.

**Tate's thesis as template.** The QNFO programme note [9] proposes that Tate's 1950 thesis provides an explicit structural template for constructing adelic quantum mechanics, records that the Dragovich programme has realized this template for free theories, and cites Huang, Stoica, and Zhong (2022) as an independent proof-of-concept in conformal field theory; it further interprets the Langlands programme in this light. This is the closest antecedent to our conjecture: it asserts local-global structure for quantum mechanics with the Archimedean place emerging as an "artifact" of the global object. Our contribution is to convert that structural claim into checkable instruments (the product formula, the completed zeta identity, tail control) and to state falsification criteria.

The companion note [11] supplies the mathematical grounding: Ostrowski's theorem partitions all non-trivial completions of $\mathbb{Q}$ into exactly one Archimedean completion ($\mathbb{R}$, the $\infty$-place) and infinitely many non-Archimedean completions ($\mathbb{Q}_p$ for each prime $p$), and these topologies are mutually singular — no sequence converges simultaneously in two of them. Mutual singularity is the sharp statement of the "completion problem": a sequence of rationals can have physically distinct limiting behaviors depending on which completion is used to judge convergence, so a theory that uses only one completion is making an unexamined choice. The note [12] adds a three-axis ontology — Depth (computable reals), Breadth (non-computable reals, argued to be physically vacuous), and Valuation ($p$-adic completions as information carriers) — with falsifiable predications for quantum error correction and gauge structure. Our programme adopts the Valuation axis as its working hypothesis and treats the QEC and gauge predications as downstream targets. The note [10] (Ratio-Based Adelic Physics) is supplied with an empty summary; the summary text gives no further detail, so we can relate it to our argument only through its title, which suggests that ratios of adelic norms, rather than absolute values at single places, carry the physical content — precisely the pattern our product-formula instrument (Section 4, Derivation 1) instantiates.

**Quantum dynamics and hardware.** The hybrid classical–quantum dynamics work [8] proves that consistent dynamics coupling classical and quantum degrees of freedom exists, provided it is stochastic; this dynamics is linear in the hybrid state, completely positive and trace preserving, and is applied to the back-reaction of quantum fields on spacetime without the pathologies of semi-classical equations, with several toy models introduced. The lesson for our programme is that the dynamical frame of quantum theory is already more flexible than pure real-unitary evolution: consistency can force stochasticity. An adelic factorization may similarly force modifications of the local (real) dynamics as the price of global coherence, and [8] shows that such modifications can be mathematically consistent.

The quantum sawtooth map work [7] simulates a complex dynamical system — the quantum sawtooth map model — on a quantum computer, showing that a quantum computer can efficiently extract relevant physical information, including simulating the dynamical localization of classical chaos and extracting the localization length with quadratic speedup over classical simulation. This is the methodological precedent for our proposed numerical test (3): dynamical quantities of quantum systems (localization lengths, spectral statistics) are extractable, so an adelic spectral identity constraining spectra is in principle testable on quantum hardware.

The Tianyan cloud platform work [2] reports a cloud-accessible superconducting quantum prototype, Tianyan-287, with 105 qubits and operational fidelities of 99.90% (single-qubit gates), 99.56% (two-qubit gates), and 98.7% (readout), demonstrating quantum-advantage capabilities with a Zuchongzhi 3.0-like processor. We use these stated fidelities in Section 4 as the concrete hardware baseline against which any adelic-induced spectral deviation would have to be resolved.

**Analogical and methodological neighbors.** Two further works supply methodological analogies only. The Dynamic Mode Decomposition feasibility study [3] concerns the decomposition of high-dimensional nonlinear systems into periodically distinct constituents on reduced-order subspaces via a Koopman-based technique, with a parametric study of data sampling, range, resolution, and universal convergence states; the analogy to our programme is the decomposition of dynamics into constituents, though the summary supplied gives no adelic content. The polyhedral-symmetry work in social choice [5] computes probabilities of election outcomes by counting integral points in polyhedra using Ehrhart theory, noting that dimension and complexity of the involved polyhedra grow rapidly; the analogy is that arithmetic counting techniques can compute probabilities in seemingly non-arithmetic settings, but the summary supplied gives no direct connection to quantum or adelic physics, and we do not rely on it.

## 3. Methods

### 3.1 The completion problem and the adelic ring

Let $\mathbb{Q}$ carry, at the place $\infty$, the usual absolute value $|x|_\infty$, and at each prime $p$ the $p$-adic absolute value $|x|_p = p^{-v_p(x)}$, where $v_p(x)$ is the exponent of $p$ in the prime factorization of $x$. Ostrowski's theorem states these are all the non-trivial completions [11]. The adele ring $\mathbb{A}_\mathbb{Q}$ is the restricted product $\mathbb{R} \times \prod_p{}' \mathbb{Q}_p$, in which all but finitely many components lie in the ring of $p$-adic integers.

### 3.2 The factorization conjecture

**Conjecture (Adelic factorization of quantum dynamics).** There is a formulation of quantum dynamics in which the dynamical object (evolution operator, path integral, or spectral zeta function) factorizes as

$$\mathcal{D} = \mathcal{D}_\infty \cdot \prod_{p} \mathcal{D}_p,$$

where $\mathcal{D}_\infty$ is the standard real/complex quantum dynamics and each $\mathcal{D}_p$ is a local factor at the $p$-adic completion, subject to a global consistency condition analogous to the zeta functional equation.

The conjecture is *structural*, not yet dynamical: we do not specify the local factors' Hilbert spaces or Hamiltonians. Following [9], the template is Tate's thesis, in which local zeta integrals at every place combine into a global object whose functional equation is a theorem, not an assumption. Following [6], the required adelic Lie-theoretic apparatus (exponential maps, exact sequences) exists. Following [1] and [4], adelic measures and adelic line bundles already support rigidity and equidistribution theorems in arithmetic dynamics, so the pattern "local data at every place + global condition $\Rightarrow$ strong constraints" is established mathematics.
