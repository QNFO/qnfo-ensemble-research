## Research idea

Adelic Factorization of Quantum Dynamics Across All Completions of Q. Conjecture: the choice of the Archimedean completion ℝ as the number field underlying quantum theory is a convention, not a necessity; Ostrowski's theorem permits p-adic completions, and a physically consistent formulation should factorize into local contributions at every place of ℚ. If quantum dynamics admits local factors at each completion (∞, 2, 3, 5, …), global consistency conditions—by analogy with Tate's thesis, where the zeta functional equation emerges from adelic Poisson summation—should force an adelic product structure on the theory. This matters because it would explain why ℝ appears in physics and predict observable or structural consequences of the finite-prime factors. Testable via: (1) formal derivation of an adelic path integral or spectral zeta identity whose functional equation constrains physical amplitudes; (2) constructing p-adic quantum models and checking whether their adelic product recovers standard real-valued QM in an appropriate limit; (3) numerical study of adelic zeta identities as consistency conditions on spectra.
