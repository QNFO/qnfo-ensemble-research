## Research idea

P-adic ancestry model: quantum correlation without co-existing worlds. The author conjectures that p-adic/ultrametric branching should be read as a fixed ancestral (family-tree) structure encoding present correlations, not as co-existing future worlds: entangled particles share a 'common ancestor' node in a hierarchical state-space, so Bell-type correlations arise from nested history rather than superluminal influence or Many-Worlds branching. This matters because it offers a locally deterministic, single-branch ontology that still explains nonlocal correlation. A paper could test it by constructing an explicit ultrametric hidden-variable model on a p-adic tree, deriving its correlation functions, and checking whether it reproduces (or provably cannot reproduce) the Tsirelson bound and Bell-inequality violations; simulations could compare predicted correlation patterns against EPR-Bohm data.
