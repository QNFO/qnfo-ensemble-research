## Research idea

Bruhat-Tits tree encodes particle mass spectrum. Conjecture: the Bruhat–Tits tree of Qp admits a natural spectral decomposition whose eigenvalue ratios reproduce (or predict) physical mass ratios, extending the p-adic holography programme (Chen–Liu–Hung) from black-hole geometry to particle spectra. The author claims this encoding is absent from the Dragovich and Hung literatures and is a genuinely new bridge between p-adic geometry and phenomenology. Testability: (1) formally define a Laplacian on the Bruhat–Tits tree with boundary conditions motivated by the adelic product formula and compute its point spectrum; (2) numerically compare the resulting eigenvalue hierarchy against Standard Model mass ratios and the p-adic minimum length 1/p from Zúñiga–Galindo's p-adic Dirac analysis; (3) check whether adelic invariance (product over all places) constrains the spectrum in a way that fixes otherwise free parameters. A successful derivation would give number-theoretic output predicting physics, mirroring the Huang–Stoica–Zhong adelic CFT proof-of-concept for quadratic reciprocity.
