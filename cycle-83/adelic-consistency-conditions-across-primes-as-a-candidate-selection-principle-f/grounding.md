## Research idea

Adelic consistency conditions across all primes fix physical constants. The note conjectures that the missing information behind the three constants problem—the fine-structure constant's exact value, lepton mass hierarchy, and absolute scale—is encoded not in the real continuum but in the p-adic places of an adelic structure. The real line is treated as one leaf of an adelic foliation, with other leaves being ultrametric Bruhat–Tits trees; particle stability then requires consistency conditions satisfied at every prime simultaneously. A paper could test this by formulating a variational or fixed-point principle on the adèle ring A_Q whose stationary points reproduce α and lepton mass ratios, checking whether the adelic product formula imposes quantization conditions matching observed spectra, and simulating ultrametric dynamics on Bruhat–Tits trees to see if cross-prime closure selects the observed values.
