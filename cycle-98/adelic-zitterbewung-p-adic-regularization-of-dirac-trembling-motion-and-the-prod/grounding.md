## Research idea

Adelic zitterbewegung: p-adic regularization of Dirac trembling motion at short scales. Claim: formulating the free Dirac equation over the adele ring A = R × ∏Q_p yields a zitterbewegung oscillating simultaneously in the real and all p-adic sectors; by the adele product formula the p-adic contributions cancel at macroscopic scales, recovering Schrödinger's standard oscillation at ω = 2mc²/ħ, while near the Compton/Planck length non-archimedean terms dominate, discretizing or fractalizing the motion and preventing singularities. Why it matters: this gives a concrete mechanism whereby adelic geometry regularizes relativistic quantum dynamics and modifies the path integral at short distances, potentially producing observable deviations. Test: derive the adelic Dirac equation and velocity (α-matrix) expectation values sector by sector; prove or numerically demonstrate the product-formula cancellation; simulate wave-packet dynamics on Q_p (Vladimirov operator, p-adic gamma matrices) to exhibit discrete/fractal trembling and quantify scale-dependent frequency shifts; compare the resulting regularized path integral with the standard real formulation.
