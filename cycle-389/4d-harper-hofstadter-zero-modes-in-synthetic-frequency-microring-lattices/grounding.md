## Research idea

4D Harper-Hofstadter zero-modes in synthetic-frequency microring lattices. Conjecture: a 10x10 array of ultra-high-Q microring resonators on hybrid SiN/LNOI, with EOMs driven at the FSR to synthesize spectral hopping, realizes a 4D Harper-Hofstadter Hamiltonian whose frequency-comb 'edge' supports a chiral zero-mode. The claim to test: this mode's unidirectional propagation is geometrically protected—backscattering forbidden by lattice topology—so it persists under induced local disorder, with a non-trivial second Chern number C2≠0 as the invariant signature. Why it matters: it would demonstrate genuine topological protection in a synthetic (spectral) dimension rather than software-corrected robustness, enabling robust on-chip frequency-comb transport. How to test: derive the band structure and Chern number from the driven-resonator tight-binding model; simulate wavepacket dynamics under disorder to verify zero-mode localization and chirality; predict experimental observables (spectral-resolved transmission, mode decay rates) distinguishing topological protection from trivial localization.
