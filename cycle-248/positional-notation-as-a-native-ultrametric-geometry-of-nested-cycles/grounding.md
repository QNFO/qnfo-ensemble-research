## Research idea

Positional Notation as Native Ultrametric Geometry of Nested Cycles. Claim: positional notation intrinsically encodes a tree of nested cycles, and the b-adic (ultrametric) distance between numerals — b^{-k} for k shared trailing digits — is not an artifact but the natural metric of the representation, matching phenomenal groupings (e.g., calendar dates as mixed-radix numerals whose shared-prefix distance is causally more relevant than linear day-count). The 'silent radix' default of decimal, dissolved into an Archimedean line, erases this structure; re-founding representation on explicit bases restores an ultrametric native to both numeral and measured world. Testable: (1) formally prove the isomorphism between place-value carrying, nested distinction-marking (Laws of Form re-entry), and p-adic valuation trees; (2) simulate periodic phenomena (seasonal, astronomical) and compare predictive clustering under shared-prefix ultrametric versus Euclidean timeline distance; (3) characterize which real measurement domains satisfy the ultrametric inequality better than linear metrics, establishing when base-matched representation is empirically superior.
