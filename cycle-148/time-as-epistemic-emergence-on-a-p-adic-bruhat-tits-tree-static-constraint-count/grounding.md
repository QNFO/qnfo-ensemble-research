## Research idea

Time as epistemic emergence on a p-adic Bruhat-Tits tree. Conjecture: fundamental reality is a timeless, static p-adic Bruhat-Tits tree whose quantum state satisfies the Wheeler-DeWitt constraint HΨ=0; the experienced flow of time is an emergent, observer-generated cognitive fiction arising from a subsystem's sequential traversal of tree nodes. This dissolves the problem of time in quantum gravity by replacing the spacetime manifold with non-Archimedean branching geometry and relegating dynamics to epistemology. Testable program: (1) formulate a Wheeler-DeWitt-type constraint on a p-adic Bruhat-Tits tree and show its solution space admits subsystem 'records' whose correlations define a thermodynamic arrow; (2) derive an emergent semiclassical Hamiltonian as an approximation to node-to-node amplitude structure, recovering real-line time in a large-p or continuum limit; (3) simulate simple observer models on the tree to show linear narrative ordering emerges as an optimal predictive compression of branching structure, connecting to evolutionary epistemology.

  § 6. Discussion: **Limitations.** The model as constructed is a kinematic skeleton: it fixes ordering and counting but assigns no dynamics. The identification $t \leftrightarrow v_p(x)$ is a conjecture, not a derivation; nothing in the combinatorics above forces the root to be a cosmological origin or one depth step to be a Planck time (Projection P2 is explicitly assumption-laden). The prime $p$ is a free parameter with no observed value; the $p=3$ phase-transition threshold found in the Potts analysis [3] is the only hint in the surveyed literature that specific primes may be dynamically distinguished, and that result concerns a Potts system on a Cayley tree, not temporal ordering. The model also has no spatial degrees of freedom: the light-cone question is answered only in the limited sense of cone counting (Section 4.8), not by a derivation of relativistic light-cone structure. **Failure modes.** The most serious structural mismatch is with relativity.   § 7. Conclusion: We have formalized a conjectural model in which time is the depth coordinate $d(v) \in \mathbb{N}$ on a rooted $(p+1)$-regular Bruhat-Tits tree, conjecturally identified with the $p$-adic valuation $v_p(x)$. The model's virtues are that discreteness (integer ticks $t_0$), partial temporal ordering (ancestry on the tree, with incomparability across branches), and acyclicity of causality (Claim 4.6) are theorems of the structure rather than added assumptions. The exact combinatorial consequences computed here — $V_{10} = 3070$ events in the depth-$10$ ball for $p=2$, $C_{10} = 27651$ ordered pairs out of $\binom{3070}{2} = 4{,}710{,}915$, a comparability fraction $f_{10} \approx 5.8696 \times 10^{-3}$ with asymptotics $f_D \sim D/p^{D} \to 0$, mean depth $\bar{d} = 27651/3070 \approx 9.0068$, and future-cone counts $C(5) = 62$ ($p=2$) and $363$ ($p=3$) — are all derived with shown arithmetic and require no simulation.
