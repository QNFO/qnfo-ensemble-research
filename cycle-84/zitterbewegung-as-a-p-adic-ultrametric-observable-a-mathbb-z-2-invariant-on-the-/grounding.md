## Research idea

Zitterbewegung as a p-adic ultrametric observable. Conjecture: zitterbewegung is best understood not as an Archimedean oscillation but as a p-adic observable whose readout is ultrametric (Bruhat–Tits tree connectivity), with a ℤ₂-valued topological invariant carried by the current correlator. The claim is that the Compton-frequency beat between positive- and negative-energy branches admits an adelic formulation in which the 'oscillation' is reinterpreted as hierarchical, non-Archimedean connectivity, and that this reframing yields a measurable signature. Why it matters: it would extend the standard Hilbert-space decomposition of ZBW (spectral gap + Clifford algebra origin + Newton–Wigner no-go) into the adelic regime, connecting relativistic quantum dynamics to p-adic physics and topological quantum computing (Majorana platforms). Test: (1) derive the p-adic analogue of the Dirac spectral decomposition and show the ℤ₂ invariant is well-defined and conserved under the ultrametric evolution; (2) compute the current correlator in both Archimedean and p-adic pictures and identify a distinguishing observable; (3) propose detection via spin noise spectroscopy, EELS/RIXS, or a Gromov δ-type measurement in topological materials hosting Majorana modes, predicting a discrete ℤ₂ signature rather than a continuous oscillation.
