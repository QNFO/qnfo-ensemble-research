## Research idea

Discrete Bruhat–Tits Tree Analog of Kapustin–Witten S-Duality. The author conjectures that the Kapustin–Witten construction—where S-duality of N=4 SYM under the GL twist yields geometric Langlands—has a p-adic counterpart obtained by replacing the Riemann surface C with P¹(Q_p) and the continuous gauge theory with a discrete model on the Bruhat–Tits tree T_p, with Wilson/'t Hooft operator exchange mapping to a tree-level Langlands correspondence. This matters because it would extend the physics-derived consilience from the Archimedean to the non-Archimedean setting, suggesting Langlands is a unified physical duality across places. A paper could test this by defining a discrete gauge theory on T_p with loop operators on edges, constructing an S-duality-like involution exchanging electric and magnetic data, and checking whether harmonic functions on the tree (p-adic analogs of modular forms) arise as eigensheaves/eigenfunctions under the induced Hecke operators, comparing with known p-adic geometric Langlands statements.
