## Research idea

L-functions as gauge-invariant spectra across tree, hyperbolic, and p-adic geometries. The note conjectures that the same L-function (e.g., L(s,θ) for the Gaussian theta series) serves as a single invariant object appearing simultaneously as an ultrametric invariant on Bruhat–Tits trees, a cross-ratio/resonance spectrum on the Archimedean hyperbolic plane, and a cophenetic distance on the prime-factorization hierarchy. The choice of field (R vs Q_p) is a 'gauge choice' changing the visible geometry, while the zeros of the L-function remain universal spectral invariants governing harmonic resonance in all three pictures. This matters because it would give a concrete, computable mechanism for how discrete hierarchical structure generates continuous geometric reality, connecting ultrametric geometry, modular forms, and the Langlands program. A paper could test this by: (1) computing theta-function values at roots of unity q=e^{2πia/b} and verifying that interference peaks align with Farey fractions as claimed; (2) explicitly deriving the cocycle by which the Minkowski question-mark map transforms cross-ratios when conjugating PGL(2,Z) action to Thompson group F; (3) numerically comparing tree-Laplacian eigenvalues on Bruhat–Tits trees with Maass form eigenvalues to check whether L-function zeros truly govern both spectra.
