## Research idea

Finite-prime valuations as necessary, not anthropocentric, in fundamental physics. The note conjectures that privileging the real numbers (the archimedean completion of ℚ) is the true anthropocentric bias, while treating all completions of ℚ democratically—adelic physics—may reveal that finite primes (2, 3, 5, …) enter quantization conditions, scattering amplitudes, and entropy formulas in ways that are mathematically forced rather than chosen. A paper could test this by: (1) deriving whether known quantization/entropy formulas can be formulated purely adelically, with the archimedean place demoted to one among many; (2) running computational searches for p-adic/adelic structures in established results (e.g., p-adic string theory amplitudes, adelic product formulas) to check whether finite-prime contributions are unavoidable or removable; (3) formally asking whether any valuation-free substrate (idempotent semirings, quantum logic) can recover known physics without covertly reintroducing prime valuations. If finite primes are forced, arithmetic is woven into nature beyond continuum bias.
