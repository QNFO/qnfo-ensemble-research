Omics data discovery agents: Agent-supported retrieval, reanalysis, and synthesis of published omics data. <p>by Alexandre Hutton, Jesse G. Meyer</p> The biomedical literature contains a vast collection of omics studies, yet most published data remain functionally inaccessible for computational reuse. When raw data are deposited in public repositories, essential information for reproducing reported results is dispersed across main text, supplementary files, and code repositories, and in the rarer cases where intermediate data (e.g., protein abundance files) are shared, their location is irregular.
