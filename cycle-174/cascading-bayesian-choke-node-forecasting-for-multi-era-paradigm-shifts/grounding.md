## Research idea

Cascading Bayesian Choke-Node Forecasting for Multi-Era Paradigm Shifts. The author conjectures that long-horizon forecasting of technological or scientific paradigm shifts is best modeled not as independent milestone estimates but as a Markov chain of eras, where each era's outcome distribution is explicitly conditioned on the previous era's state (optimistic/baseline/pessimistic), and where a small set of 'choke nodes' — conditional probability pairs with maximal leverage on total lifecycle expected value — can be identified and stress-tested via adversarial 'leverage shift' updates. This matters because most foresight methods treat milestones independently, ignoring cascading dependency structure and sensitivity to specific transitions. A paper could formalize the framework: derive marginal distributions via matrix multiplication of conditional transition matrices, prove or bound how choke-node identification (e.g., via derivative of total EV with respect to each conditional entry) ranks transitions, and validate via Monte Carlo simulation on historical roadmaps (e.g., solid-state batteries, fusion) to test whether the cascading model's EV predictions outperform independent-era baselines.
