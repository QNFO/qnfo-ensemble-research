## Research idea

Singularity as Epistemic Artifact: Cyclical Cosmology Without Actual Infinities. The note conjectures that black-hole singularities and infinite quantities (zero, infinity, Hilbert's-hotel-style infinities) are artifacts of adopting a linear number line for quantitative physics, rather than features of reality; in a cyclical-cosmology framework, a 'singularity' marks a transition between epochs, not a true infinity. Why it matters: if correct, the singularity theorems' divergences signal model inadequacy, and Penrose-style cyclic models could be reformulated with finite, constructively defined quantities. Test: formally analyze which singularity-theorem assumptions (geodesic completeness, energy conditions) require actual infinite sets versus finite cyclic structures; build a discrete or circular-number-line toy model of gravitational collapse and simulate whether curvature maxima remain finite while still producing Penrose-style conformal cyclic boundaries; compare predictions (e.g., Hawking points, entropy transfer between aeons) against standard singular GR.
