## Research idea

Real Numbers as Coarse-Grained Projections of Re-Entry-Generated p-adic Trees. The note conjectures that extending Spencer-Brown's calculus of indications with re-entry (self-referential boundaries, e.g. f = ¬f) generates structures isomorphic to p-adic integers, and that the real line is a lossy, metric-imposed projection of these ultrametric boundary trees. Testable program: (1) formalize the re-entry calculus as a rewriting system and prove (or refute) an isomorphism between its normal forms and Z_p; (2) define an explicit coarse-graining map from p-adic trees to reals and characterize which topological/metric properties of R survive the projection; (3) simulate normalization sequences computationally to check whether real-number arithmetic emerges as a quotient of boundary manipulations. If successful, this would ground real analysis in a minimal distinction-based foundation and clarify whether such a calculus could rival ZFC for constructive, computationally verifiable mathematics.
